怎么用这三个级别How to use the three levels

  • 同一道题，我们写了三个级别的示范：5–6 级、7–8 级、9 级以上。内容骨架一样，差别只在四个评分维度上一级一级往上加。先找到和自己最像的那一栏，再看下一栏把哪几句换掉了。For every prompt we wrote three versions: Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+. The skeleton is the same; each level adds to the four rating dimensions. Find the column that sounds most like you, then see which sentences the next column changes.
  • 5–6 级示范是「干净版」：真实的 5–6 级录音里还会有停顿、重说和一些小语法错，我们没有故意写错，免得你把错的学进去。你自己的录音如果更短、更断，那正是这一级常见的样子。The Level 5–6 versions are clean: real Level 5–6 speech also has pauses, restarts and small grammar slips, but we did not write mistakes in, so you will not learn them. If your own recording is shorter and choppier, that is normal for this level.
  • 这些都是我们写的示范，不是官方样本，也没有经过官方评分员打分。级别是按官方四个维度和成绩单上 5 级、9 级的描述来写的。These are our own examples, not official samples, and no official rater has scored them. The levels follow the official four dimensions and the Level 5 and Level 9 score-report descriptions.

What Level 9+ adds is density, not speed.

5–6 级长什么样What Level 5–6 looks like

  • 会用 will 预测，但每个预测只有一句，没有理由。Uses will to predict, but each prediction is one sentence with no reason.
  • 一直重复 I think … will …。Keeps repeating "I think ... will ...".
  • 人物指得不清楚（the boy、the man），听者不确定说的是谁。Vague references (the boy, the man), so the listener is unsure who is meant.

7–8 级长什么样What Level 7–8 looks like

  • 每个预测后面有 because，根据来自图里看得到的线索。Each prediction has a because, based on clues in the picture.
  • 混用 will、probably、might、going to。Mixes will, probably, might and going to.
  • 先说清是哪个人（the woman who is looking at her watch），再预测。Identifies the person first (the woman who is looking at her watch), then predicts.

9 级以上长什么样What Level 9+ looks like

  • 预测有把握程度的层次：I suspect、is bound to、I wouldn't be surprised if。Predictions are graded by certainty: I suspect, is bound to, I wouldn't be surprised if.
  • 预测连成一条故事线，人物之间有互动和反应，像官方 9 级样本还猜到了家长怎么回应。Predictions form a storyline with people reacting to each other, as the official Level 9 sample guessed how the parent would respond.
  • 用 as soon as、until、once 把时间顺序说清楚。As soon as, until and once make the timing clear.

为什么常卡在 7 级Why people get stuck at 7

  • 在描述现在（任务 3 的做法），而不是预测接下来。Describes the present (Task 3 habits) instead of predicting what comes next.
  • 所有预测都是 will，没有「可能」「肯定」的区别。Every prediction uses will, with no difference between likely and certain.
  • 预测天马行空，和图里的线索没关系。Predictions are random, not linked to clues in the picture.

冲 9 级以上的三个动作Three moves to reach 9+

  1. 每个预测用一个「把握词」：probably / I suspect / is bound to。Give each prediction a certainty word: probably, I suspect, is bound to.
  2. 至少一个预测写成「A 做了什么→B 会怎么反应」。Make at least one prediction a chain: A does something, B reacts.
  3. 结尾说这一幕最后会怎样收场。End with how the whole scene will wrap up.

拿第 1 题对照看Side by side: prompt 1

A farmers' market on a sunny Saturday morning. A woman is buying apples from a smiling seller. An older man holds a cloth bag and tastes a piece of cheese. A girl holds a balloon. A dog waits next to a table of flowers. Colourful tents and a few musicians in the background.

Level 5–65–6 级 · 66 words66 词

I think the woman will pay for the apples. Then she will go to the flowers. She wants to buy more things.

The girl has a balloon. I think the balloon will fly away. She will cry. The old man will help her.

The dog will eat something from the table. The seller will laugh.

At the end, everyone will go home happy with fresh food.

Level 7–87–8 级 · 119 words119 词

I think the woman will pay for her apples and put them in her bag. Then she will probably walk to the flower table, because she looks like she wants to buy something else.

The little girl is holding her balloon with one hand. I think it might fly away soon. She will feel sad and maybe cry, but the older man will probably help her. He is tall, so he can catch the string.

The dog is waiting near the flowers. It might eat something from the table, and the seller will laugh and give it a small piece of cheese.

In the end, I think everyone will go home happy with their bags full of fresh food.

Level 9+9 级以上 · 139 words139 词

I imagine the woman will pay for her apples, tuck them away and wander over to the flower stall, since she seems to be in the mood to browse a bit longer.

As for the little girl, she's only loosely holding her balloon, so I suspect it's going to slip out of her hand any second now. She'll probably be on the verge of tears, but the older man, being quite tall, will most likely reach up, grab the string and hand it back to her.

Meanwhile, the dog by the flowers looks like it's eyeing the food. I wouldn't be surprised if it snatched a bite of something, and the vendor, rather than getting annoyed, will laugh and toss it a scrap of cheese.

By noon, I'd expect everyone to head home with full bags and a smile.

Level 5–6 → 7–8从 5–6 级到 7–8 级

  1. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 Then she will go to the flowers. She wants to buy more things. → Then she will probably walk to the flower table, because she looks like she wants to buy something else.
    because 加上从图里看到的根据，预测就有了理由。"Because" plus a clue from the picture gives the prediction a reason.
  2. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 She will cry. The old man will help her. → She will feel sad and maybe cry, but the older man will probably help her. He is tall, so he can catch the string.
    说清老人为什么能帮（个子高），预测更可信。Explaining why the man can help (he is tall) makes the prediction believable.

Level 7–8 → 9+从 7–8 级到 9 级以上

  1. Vocabulary词汇 I think it might fly away soon. → I suspect it's going to slip out of her hand any second now
    I suspect、any second now 换掉了反复的 I think、soon，表达把握程度更细。"I suspect" and "any second now" replace the repeated "I think" and "soon", and grade certainty more finely.
  2. Listenability好懂程度 He is tall, so he can catch the string. → the older man, being quite tall, will most likely reach up, grab the string and hand it back to her
    插入的分词短语 being quite tall 说明原因，后面三个动词连用，节奏紧凑。The participle phrase "being quite tall" gives the reason, and three verbs in a row keep the pace.
  3. Listenability好懂程度 It might eat something from the table, and the seller will laugh → I wouldn't be surprised if it snatched a bite of something, and the vendor, rather than getting annoyed, will laugh
    I wouldn't be surprised if 是委婉的预测句型，rather than 插入对比。"I wouldn't be surprised if" is a soft prediction pattern, and "rather than" adds a contrast.
