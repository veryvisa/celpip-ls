        官方怎么讲这个任务What the official packs say about this task

        回答一个「你认为……吗？」的问题，说出你的观点。准备 30 秒，说 90 秒。Give your opinion on a question that starts “Do you think…?”. 30 s to prepare, 90 s to speak.
        • 仔细读题，回答不要跑题。Read the question carefully and stay on topic.
        • 很快决定答「是」还是「否」。Decide quickly: yes or no.
        • 给大约三个理由。Give about three reasons.
        • 每个理由都有具体细节。Support each reason with specific details.
        • 有逻辑地排列，换新想法时用过渡词提示。Order your ideas logically and mark each new one with a transition.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        What worked做得好的地方

        高分样本多做了什么What the higher-level samples did differently

        • 8 级样本的教训最值钱：词汇再好，立场前后矛盾也上不去。选定一边，从头说到尾；想承认反方，就用一句「虽然……但是……」带过。The Level 8 sample teaches the most: great vocabulary cannot save an answer that contradicts itself. Pick a side and keep it; if you want to admit the other side, do it in one “although…, …” sentence.
        • 同一道第二语言题：5 级的理由很空（世界变小了）；9 级落到加拿大的具体好处（法语、联邦工作、工资）。具体到本地，就是最便宜的加分。Same second-language question: Level 5’s reasons are abstract (the world is getting smaller); Level 9 brings them down to concrete Canadian benefits (French, federal jobs, pay). Local, concrete detail is the cheapest way up.

        这个任务要你做什么What this task wants

        回答一个「你认为……吗？」的问题：给出你的观点并支持它。Answer a “Do you think…?” question: state your opinion and support it.
        • 先看清楚问题，别跑题。Yes 或 No 都行，选你更容易讲的。Read carefully and stay on topic; yes or no is fine.
        • 大约三个理由，每个理由配具体细节。About three reasons, each with a detail.
        • 用 First / In addition / Finally 串起来。Use transitions to signal new ideas.

        官方时间：准备 30 秒，说话 90 秒。准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳转并发出提示音，录音自动开始。Official timing: 30 s prep and 90 s speaking. When prep ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear a prompt, and recording starts automatically.

        • 别纠结立场，选你更容易举例的那边。Don't agonize over your position. Pick the side where it is easier for you to give examples.
        • 理由要「分开」：不要三个理由其实说的是同一件事。Keep the reasons "separate": don't give three reasons that are really about the same thing.
        • 90 秒不短：每个理由大约 25 秒，加上开头和结尾。90 seconds is not short: about 25 seconds per reason, plus the opening and ending.
