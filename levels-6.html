怎么用这三个级别How to use the three levels

  • 同一道题，我们写了三个级别的示范：5–6 级、7–8 级、9 级以上。内容骨架一样，差别只在四个评分维度上一级一级往上加。先找到和自己最像的那一栏，再看下一栏把哪几句换掉了。For every prompt we wrote three versions: Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+. The skeleton is the same; each level adds to the four rating dimensions. Find the column that sounds most like you, then see which sentences the next column changes.
  • 5–6 级示范是「干净版」：真实的 5–6 级录音里还会有停顿、重说和一些小语法错，我们没有故意写错，免得你把错的学进去。你自己的录音如果更短、更断，那正是这一级常见的样子。The Level 5–6 versions are clean: real Level 5–6 speech also has pauses, restarts and small grammar slips, but we did not write mistakes in, so you will not learn them. If your own recording is shorter and choppier, that is normal for this level.
  • 这些都是我们写的示范，不是官方样本，也没有经过官方评分员打分。级别是按官方四个维度和成绩单上 5 级、9 级的描述来写的。These are our own examples, not official samples, and no official rater has scored them. The levels follow the official four dimensions and the Level 5 and Level 9 score-report descriptions.

5–6 级长什么样What Level 5–6 looks like

  • 说清了问题和要求，但很直接（I want…、Please give me…），听起来有点硬。States the problem and the request, but bluntly (I want..., Please give me...), which can sound harsh.
  • 理由短，没有具体事实（日期、次数、金额）。Short reasons with no concrete facts (dates, times, amounts).
  • 很少给替代方案。Rarely offers an alternative.

7–8 级长什么样What Level 7–8 looks like

  • 用 Could you please、I'd really appreciate 等礼貌句型。Uses polite patterns such as Could you please and I'd really appreciate.
  • 给出具体事实（三周、两次、六十八元）。Gives concrete facts (three weeks, twice, sixty-eight dollars).
  • 拒绝时给一到两个替代方案；投诉时说清希望的结果。Offers one or two alternatives when refusing; states the outcome wanted when complaining.

9 级以上长什么样What Level 9+ looks like

  • 语气「坚定但给面子」：mix-up、glitch 代替 mistake，先表达愿意私下解决再说后果。Firm but face-saving: mix-up or glitch instead of mistake, and an offer to settle privately before the consequence.
  • 从对方角度说话（whichever is easier on your end），主动揽下后续。Speaks from the listener's side (whichever is easier on your end) and offers to handle the next step.
  • 后果有明确期限，用 have no choice but to 显得不得已。Consequences have a clear deadline, and "have no choice but to" makes them sound unavoidable.

为什么常卡在 7 级Why people get stuck at 7

  • 语气不对：对房东、经理太随便，对朋友太正式。官方 8 级样本就因为结尾说了 Thanks for listening 被扣分。Wrong tone: too casual with a landlord or manager, too formal with a friend. The official Level 8 sample lost marks for ending with "Thanks for listening".
  • 只说问题，不说希望对方做什么。Describes the problem but not what you want done.
  • 立场前后摇摆，一会儿拒绝一会儿又像答应。The position wobbles between refusing and agreeing.

冲 9 级以上的三个动作Three moves to reach 9+

  1. 开口前定好三件事：对谁说、要什么、给什么替代方案。Before speaking, decide three things: who you are talking to, what you want, what alternative you offer.
  2. 准备两个「软化」的词：mix-up、I'd rather、I'm afraid。Keep two softeners ready: mix-up, I'd rather, I'm afraid.
  3. 结尾用一句行动邀请（Just let me know…），不要用 Thanks for listening。End with a call to action (Just let me know...), not "Thanks for listening".

拿第 1 题对照看Side by side: prompt 1

You bought a coffee machine online, but it stopped working after a week. Call the store and explain what you want.

Level 5–65–6 级 · 70 words70 词

Hello, my name is Wendy Chen. I bought a coffee machine on your website.

It stopped working after one week. This morning I turned it on, but nothing happened. I checked the plug, and I tried another plug. It still doesn't work.

I want a refund or a new machine. I have the receipt and the box. I like your store.

What can I do? Please help me. Thank you.

Level 7–87–8 级 · 115 words115 词

Hello, my name is Wendy Chen. I'm calling about a coffee machine that I bought on your website last week.

The problem is that it stopped working after only seven days. I turned it on this morning, and nothing happened. I checked the plug, and I tried a different outlet, but it's still not working.

I like your store, so I'd like to solve this in a simple way. I would like a refund, or you can send me a new machine. Either one is fine for me. I still have the receipt and the box.

Could you please tell me what I should do next? I'd really appreciate your help. Thank you so much.

Level 9+9 级以上 · 136 words136 词

Hello, my name is Wendy Chen. I'm calling about a coffee machine I ordered from your website last week, order number ending in 4-1-7.

Unfortunately, it stopped working after just seven days. This morning I switched it on and got absolutely nothing: no lights, no sound. I've already ruled out the obvious things; I checked the plug and tried a different outlet, but it's completely dead.

I've always had good experiences with your store, so I'm hoping we can sort this out quickly. I'd be happy with either a full refund or a replacement unit, whichever is easier on your end. I've kept the receipt and the original packaging.

Could you let me know the next steps, for example whether I should ship it back or drop it off? I'd really appreciate your help. Thank you.

Level 5–6 → 7–8从 5–6 级到 7–8 级

  1. Task Fulfillment完成任务 I want a refund or a new machine. → I would like a refund, or you can send me a new machine. Either one is fine for me.
    对店家说 I would like 比 I want 礼貌；Either one is fine 显得好商量。"I would like" is more polite to a store than "I want", and "Either one is fine" sounds flexible.
  2. Task Fulfillment完成任务 What can I do? Please help me. → Could you please tell me what I should do next? I'd really appreciate your help.
    could you please、I'd really appreciate 是投诉电话里得体的语气。"Could you please" and "I'd really appreciate" set the right tone for a complaint call.

Level 7–8 → 9+从 7–8 级到 9 级以上

  1. Vocabulary词汇 I checked the plug, and I tried a different outlet, but it's still not working. → I've already ruled out the obvious things
    rule out（排除）说明自己已经做过基本检查，让对方不必再问。"Ruled out the obvious things" shows you have done the basic checks, saving the agent's questions.
  2. Vocabulary词汇 I like your store, so I'd like to solve this in a simple way. → I've always had good experiences with your store, so I'm hoping we can sort this out quickly.
    sort this out（解决）是地道说法，先肯定对方让语气更友好。"Sort this out" is natural, and praising the store first keeps the tone friendly.
  3. Task Fulfillment完成任务 Either one is fine for me. → whichever is easier on your end
    从对方角度考虑（哪个对你们方便），9 级描述里的「根据听众调整语气」。Thinking from their side ("easier on your end") is the tone adjustment the Level 9 description mentions.
