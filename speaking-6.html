        官方怎么讲这个任务What the official packs say about this task

        向朋友、家人、熟人或顾客解释你做的一个决定（两个对象或做法二选一）。准备 60 秒，说 60 秒。Tell someone (a friend, a relative, someone you know a little, or a customer) about a decision you have made; you pick one of two options. 60 s to prepare, 60 s to speak.
        • 直接对这个人说话。Speak directly to the person.
        • 态度要considerate but firm：体贴，但立场不动摇。Be considerate but firm: kind, but do not move.
        • 说话要尊重对方。Be respectful.
        • 把问题讲清楚。Lay out the problem clearly.
        • 说出你的决定。State your decision.
        • 给出有力的理由支撑这个决定。Give strong reasons for it.

        Official level 5官方评分 5 级 Speaking Pro Target 5 study pack, p. 19官方 Target 5 学习包第 19 页
        你在宠物店工作，一对夫妻一个想买狗、一个想买猫；你要建议他们买哪一种。You work in a pet shop; a couple disagree, one wants a dog, the other a cat; recommend one.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 讲清观点，并按 first、second 给了两个理由（看家、帮人交朋友）；常见词用得恰当。States an opinion with two reasons in order with first, second (protection, meeting people); common words used well.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 细节和例子不够；停顿和嗯啊打断。Not enough detail or examples; pauses and fillers interrupt.
        • 语速慢，内容没说完整；语气应该更有说服力。Slow, so the answer is incomplete; the tone should be more persuasive.
        Official level 8官方评分 8 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 27官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 27 页
        顾客要退一件穿过一次就破了的衬衫，同事不肯退；你选择去说服同事。A customer wants to return a shirt that ripped after one wear; your co-worker refuses; you choose to talk to the co-worker.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 三个理由（质量差、以前有过先例、品牌可能有保修）；用词贴合情境；句式有变化。Three reasons (poor quality, a past case, a possible warranty); words fit the situation; varied sentences.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 有些语法错误；嗯啊和重来打断。Some grammar errors; fillers and restarts interrupt.
        • 对同事说完最后来一句「谢谢」，在这个场合很别扭。Ending with “thank you” to a co-worker sounds odd here.

        和 5 级样本同一道宠物店题（学习包里的附加样本，讲座里没放）。The same pet-shop question as the Level 5 sample (a bonus sample in the pack, not played in the webinar).

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 理由多（好照顾、安静、友善、不伤人），观点清楚，有细节和例子。Several reasons (easier to care for, quieter, friendlier, safer) with a clear position, details and examples.
        • 语气友好而坚定；语法错误少，有复杂句。Friendly but firm; few grammar errors, some complex sentences.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 没有按要求对这对夫妻直接说话，而是在谈论他们。Does not speak to the couple as instructed; talks about them instead.
        • 想用高级的过渡词，却用错了地方。Reaches for fancier linking words but uses them wrongly.

        高分样本多做了什么What the higher-level samples did differently

        • 同一道宠物店题：9 级理由更多、每个理由都有例子，所以拿了 9；但它在「完成任务」上仍被扣分，因为没有对着这对夫妻说。对谁说，是每个级别都要守住的底线。Same pet-shop question: Level 9 has more reasons, each with an example, which earns the 9; yet it still loses marks in Task Fulfillment for not speaking to the couple. Who you talk to is a rule at every level.
        • 8 级样本的「谢谢」说明：语气不只是礼貌，还要合乎关系（对同事、对顾客、对家人说法不同）。The Level 8 “thank you” shows that tone is not just politeness; it has to fit the relationship (co-worker, customer, family).

        我们的建议：怎么在本页练出来Our takeaways: how to practise it on this page

        这一部分是我们的建议，超出了官方学习包，不是官方内容。This part is our own advice, going beyond the official packs; it is not official content.
        • 模板第一句就叫对方的名字、说明来意，第二句讲问题，确保从第一秒就是「对他说」。模板收尾的 Thanks for listening 适合对顾客或上级；对同事、家人，换成 I hope you understand 更自然（8 级样本就在这里被扣）。The first line of our template names the person and says why you are talking, the second states the problem, so you speak to them from the first second. The template’s “Thanks for listening” suits a customer or a boss; with a co-worker or family, “I hope you understand” sounds more natural (that is where the Level 8 sample lost marks).
        • 60 秒准备够用：问题 → 决定 → 两个理由 → 一句缓和（比如提出一个替代办法）。60 s of prep is enough for problem, decision, two reasons and one softener (such as offering an alternative).
        • 官方建议两个选择各录一遍：录完后换另一个对象再说一次，你会发现语气要跟着变。The official advice is to record both choices: after recording, talk to the other person; you will notice the tone has to change.

        这个任务要你做什么What this task wants

        向朋友、家人、熟人或顾客说明一个问题和你的决定，要礼貌而坚定。Explain a problem and the decision you made, politely but firmly, to the person the task names (a friend, a relative, a customer…).
        • 语气：体谅但坚定（considerate but firm）。Be considerate but firm, and respectful.
        • 说清问题 → 说出你的决定 → 给有力的理由。Outline the problem, explain your decision, give reasons.
        • 直接对那个人说话（Hi Mr. Kim, …）。Speak directly to the person.

        官方时间：准备 60 秒，说话 60 秒。准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳转并发出提示音，录音自动开始。Official timing: 60 s prep and 60 s speaking. When prep ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear a prompt, and recording starts automatically.

        • 开头别直接质问。先 sorry to bother you，再说事实，最后才是决定。Don't start by questioning the person directly. First "sorry to bother you", then the facts, and only then the decision.
        • 「决定」要明确：I've decided to …，不要只抱怨。Make the "decision" clear: I've decided to ... Don't just complain.
        • 留 5 秒结尾：I hope you understand，听感更稳。Leave 5 seconds for an ending: "I hope you understand" sounds steadier.
