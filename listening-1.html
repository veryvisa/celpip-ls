常见陷阱Common question traps

  • 第一个方案不一定是最后选的The first solution is not the final one
    说话人常先提一个办法又放弃。把每个方案都记下来，再圈出最后同意的那个。Speakers often try one idea and drop it. Write every option, then circle the one they agree on.
  • 细节会被更正Details get corrected
    「周二吧……其实周三更好。」以后面那个为准。数字和日期会在对话中途改。“Let's say Tuesday … actually, Wednesday is better.” Note the later one. Numbers and days change mid-conversation.
  • 错误选项里重复了原话Repeated words in the wrong answer
    选项里逐字重复了音频里的词，常常是陷阱；正确答案多半是换种说法。A choice that repeats a word from the audio is often a trap. The right answer usually paraphrases.
  • 谁做决定？Who decides?
    帮忙的人提建议，有问题的人做决定。题目常问「有问题的人接下来会怎么做」。The helper suggests; the person with the problem decides. Questions often ask what the person with the problem will do.

记笔记方法Note-taking tips

把笔记页分成三格（三段音频各一格），每格写：问题、方案 1/2/3、最后选哪个。Split the page into three boxes, one per audio section: problem, option 1/2/3, final choice.
  • 用要点和缩写，不写整句；你的笔记没人看，不影响分数。Use short points and abbreviations. Nobody reads your notes and they do not affect your score.
  • 按顺序记：题目的顺序大致和答案在音频里出现的顺序一致。Take notes in order. Questions come in roughly the same order as the answers in the audio.
  • 考前用纸和笔练（敲键盘不是同一种体验），找到你自己的缩写系统。Practise with pen and paper before the test, and build your own abbreviation system.
  • 考场发纸和笔；需要更多纸可以举手要。You get paper and a pen at the test centre; raise your hand for more paper.
