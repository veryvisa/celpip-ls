这一部分考什么What this part tests

一位说话人，约 3 分钟，正式、事先准备好的发言：比如气候变化演讲、某项技术的影响、一个社区问题和几个解决方案。有概念、可能性和分析，用词较少见、较专业。One speaker for about 3 minutes giving a formal, prepared talk: a speech about climate change, the effects of a technology, or a community issue with several proposed solutions. It includes concepts, possibilities and analysis, and uses less common, more specialised language.句子补全；约 6 题同屏共用一段时间。这是听力最后一部分，也最难。每题都要答，不会就猜。Sentence completion; about 6 questions on one screen with a set time. The last part of the Listening test, and the hardest. Answer every question; guess if you have to.

常见陷阱Common question traps

  • 这是谁的观点？Whose opinion is it?
    说话人会转述别人的观点。每个人名后面写一句话观点和理由。The speaker reports other people's opinions. Write each name with a one-line summary of their view and reason.
  • 含蓄措辞Hedging language
    「有人认为」「可能」「理论上」不是事实。题目会考语气强弱。“Some argue”, “it may be”, “in theory”. These are not facts. Questions test the exact strength.
  • 生词Hard words
    不能查词典。用上下文判断褒贬，然后继续听。You cannot look them up. Decide if the word is positive or negative from context, and move on.
  • 结尾的总结Summary at the end
    说话人常在结尾再说一次要点，别提前松懈。The speaker often repeats the main point at the end. Do not stop listening.

记笔记方法Note-taking tips

每个人名一行：「名字：观点 + 理由」；结尾单独留一行写总结。One line per name: “Name: view + reason”. Leave one line at the end for the conclusion.
  • 用要点和缩写，不写整句；你的笔记没人看，不影响分数。Use short points and abbreviations. Nobody reads your notes and they do not affect your score.
  • 按顺序记：题目的顺序大致和答案在音频里出现的顺序一致。Take notes in order. Questions come in roughly the same order as the answers in the audio.
  • 考前用纸和笔练（敲键盘不是同一种体验），找到你自己的缩写系统。Practise with pen and paper before the test, and build your own abbreviation system.
  • 考场发纸和笔；需要更多纸可以举手要。You get paper and a pen at the test centre; raise your hand for more paper.
