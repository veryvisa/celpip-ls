常见陷阱Common question traps

  • 先报全名，后只说姓Full name, then last name only
    新闻里先说「Maria Lopez」，之后只说「Lopez」。记姓，才能跟上故事。A person is introduced as “Maria Lopez”, then called “Lopez”. Write the last name so you can follow the story.
  • 五个 W5 W's
    who、what、when、where、why：边听边各记一行。Who, what, when, where, why. Write one line for each as you hear it.
  • 数字和时间Numbers and times
    新闻里日期和数字很多，连同含义一起记。News is full of dates and amounts. Note them with their meaning.
  • 时间分配Time management
    所有题共用一个计时。先做容易的，其余猜。不要留空。All questions share one clock. Answer the easy ones first and guess the rest. Never leave blanks.

记笔记方法Note-taking tips

每行一个 W：Who / What / When / Where / Why；人名只写姓。One line per W: Who / What / When / Where / Why. Write only last names.
  • 用要点和缩写，不写整句；你的笔记没人看，不影响分数。Use short points and abbreviations. Nobody reads your notes and they do not affect your score.
  • 按顺序记：题目的顺序大致和答案在音频里出现的顺序一致。Take notes in order. Questions come in roughly the same order as the answers in the audio.
  • 考前用纸和笔练（敲键盘不是同一种体验），找到你自己的缩写系统。Practise with pen and paper before the test, and build your own abbreviation system.
  • 考场发纸和笔；需要更多纸可以举手要。You get paper and a pen at the test centre; raise your hand for more paper.
