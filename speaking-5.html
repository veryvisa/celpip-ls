        • 想好细节和理由，说明你的选择为什么更好。Find details and reasons why your choice is better.
        • 用具体的语言解释，并且在图片给的信息之外再多说一些。Use specific language, and go beyond the facts printed with the picture.

        Official level 5官方评分 5 级 Speaking Pro Target 5 study pack, p. 17官方 Target 5 学习包第 17 页
        家里要买风扇：你选的落地扇，对方提议手持小风扇。The family needs a fan: you chose the floor fan, they suggest a handheld mini fan.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 讲清偏好并给了理由（大、能搬动、不贵）；用了贴合情境的词。States a preference with reasons (large, portable, cheap); words fit the situation.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 说了不需要的话（描述屏幕上哪边是哪个）；嗯啊和重来很多。Includes things not needed (which side of the screen each option is on); many fillers and restarts.
        • 复杂句说不顺；任务是说服家人，语气却不够友好、不够有说服力。Struggles with longer sentences; the task is to win the family over, but the tone is neither warm nor persuasive enough.
        Official level 8官方评分 8 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 22官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 22 页
        和朋友合伙做生意：你选美食广场加盟店，朋友提议送货服务。Starting a business with a friend: you chose a food-court franchise, he suggests a delivery service.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 三个主要理由（会做饭、腰伤不能搬重物、启动资金），用上了卡片上的数字；直接对朋友说。Three main reasons (cooking skills, a back injury, start-up cost) using the numbers on the card; speaks to the friend directly.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 只有基础过渡词；语调平；多为简单句；最后一点被截断。Only basic transitions; flat intonation; mostly simple sentences; last idea cut off.
        • 要和朋友一起创业，听起来应该更兴奋一些。Going into business with a friend should sound more exciting.
        Official level 9官方评分 9 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 24官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 24 页
        同一道风扇题（对方提议吊扇）。The same fan question (they suggest a ceiling fan).

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 把理由和家里的实际需要连起来（风扇放地下室、价格合适、不用安装），连贯性强。Ties every reason to the family’s real need (the fan is for the basement, affordable, no installation): strong coherence.
        • 几乎没有停顿；先承认对方有道理，再转回自己的选择，友好又有说服力。Very few pauses; first grants the other side a point, then turns back to his choice: friendly but persuasive.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 句式可以更多样。Needs more sentence variety.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 理由多，而且有卡片上没写的（孩子玩小风扇可能伤手、落地扇有护罩、更耐用、插电不用电池）。Many reasons, including ones not on the card (children could hurt their hands on the mini fan, the floor fan has a guard, lasts longer, plugs in instead of batteries).
        • 语气自信、有说服力；用了 although、whereas、if、which 等多种复杂句。Confident, persuasive tone; a variety of complex sentences with although, whereas, if, which.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 一处半句重来打断了节奏。One restart interrupts the rhythm.

        高分样本多做了什么What the higher-level samples did differently

        • 同一道风扇题，三个级别一目了然：5 级把卡片上的信息念了一遍；9 级把信息和「我们家要用在哪里」连起来；12 级加上了卡片上没有的理由（孩子安全），并用 whereas 直接对比。这正是官方最后一条策略的样子。One fan question, three levels: Level 5 reads the card back; Level 9 connects the facts to where the family will use it; Level 12 adds reasons that are not on the card (children’s safety) and compares directly with whereas. That is the last official strategy in action.
        • 先承认对方一点（「吊扇放客厅确实不错」），再说「但是我们要的是……」，是 9 级样本说服力的来源。Granting the other option a point first (“the ceiling fan would suit the living room”) and then saying “but what we need is…” is where the Level 9 persuasiveness comes from.

        这个任务要你做什么What this task wants

        第 1 部分：在两个方案里选一个（不说话）。第 2 部分：说服对方——你选的比他提的新方案更好。Part 1: choose one of two options (you do not speak). Part 2: persuade someone that your choice is better than the option they suggest.
        • 第 1 部分只看、比较、选，别纠结：选你更容易讲出理由的那个。Part 1: compare, then choose the one you can talk about more easily.
        • 第 2 部分：先尊重对方的想法，再讲你的选择更好的两三个理由。Part 2: acknowledge the new idea, then give two or three reasons.
        • 理由要具体到图表里的信息（价格、距离、时间）。Use the details given: price, distance, time.

        官方时间：选择 60 秒（不说话），然后准备 60 秒、说话 60 秒。准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳转并发出提示音，录音自动开始。Official timing: 60 s to choose (no speaking), then 60 s prep and 60 s speaking. When prep ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear a prompt, and recording starts automatically.

        • 第 1 部分不说话，别浪费这 60 秒——先比较，再选，再想第二部分怎么反驳。Don't speak in Part 1. Don't waste these 60 seconds: compare first, then choose, then think about how to disagree in Part 2.
        • 选「你更容易说出理由」的那个，不是「你更喜欢」的那个。Choose the one that is easier for you to give reasons for, not the one you like better.
        • 抓住数字：price、distance、hours，直接用就是具体细节。Grab the numbers: price, distance, hours. Using them directly gives specific details.

        答案模板Answer template

        2. 理由一（用题目里的信息）2. Reason 1 (use the details)First, … is cheaper / closer / safer, because …
        3. 理由二3. Reason 2Also, … so we will …
        4. 对比对方的方案4. CompareYour idea is nice, but … would be a problem.
