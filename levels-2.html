怎么用这三个级别How to use the three levels

  • 同一道题，我们写了三个级别的示范：5–6 级、7–8 级、9 级以上。内容骨架一样，差别只在四个评分维度上一级一级往上加。先找到和自己最像的那一栏，再看下一栏把哪几句换掉了。For every prompt we wrote three versions: Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+. The skeleton is the same; each level adds to the four rating dimensions. Find the column that sounds most like you, then see which sentences the next column changes.
  • 5–6 级示范是「干净版」：真实的 5–6 级录音里还会有停顿、重说和一些小语法错，我们没有故意写错，免得你把错的学进去。你自己的录音如果更短、更断，那正是这一级常见的样子。The Level 5–6 versions are clean: real Level 5–6 speech also has pauses, restarts and small grammar slips, but we did not write mistakes in, so you will not learn them. If your own recording is shorter and choppier, that is normal for this level.
  • 这些都是我们写的示范，不是官方样本，也没有经过官方评分员打分。级别是按官方四个维度和成绩单上 5 级、9 级的描述来写的。These are our own examples, not official samples, and no official rater has scored them. The levels follow the official four dimensions and the Level 5 and Level 9 score-report descriptions.

5–6 级长什么样What Level 5–6 looks like

  • 按时间顺序讲完了，但只有事件，没有当时的感受和想法。The events are in order, but there are no feelings or thoughts at the time.
  • 几乎全是一般过去时的短句，很少用从句。Almost all short simple-past sentences, few clauses.
  • 结尾有一句「学到了什么」，但很简短。A one-line lesson at the end, very brief.

7–8 级长什么样What Level 7–8 looks like

  • 故事有起伏：铺垫（以为时间很多）→问题→怎么解决→结果。The story has a shape: set-up (I thought I had time), problem, solution, result.
  • 加了感受（panic、embarrassed、relieved）和一两句对话。Feelings (panic, embarrassed, relieved) and a line or two of dialogue.
  • 用了过去进行时、would have 等，时态更准确。Past continuous and would have: more accurate tenses.

9 级以上长什么样What Level 9+ looks like

  • 细节精挑细选，一个画面就让人身临其境（跑调的生日歌、胃里打结）。Carefully chosen details put the listener there (an off-key birthday song, a knot in the stomach).
  • 讲故事的衔接语和句型：to make matters worse、only to、not only … but also。Storytelling linkers and patterns: to make matters worse, only to, not only ... but also.
  • 结尾是一个有分量的反思，不是套话。The ending is a real reflection, not a stock phrase.

为什么常卡在 7 级Why people get stuck at 7

  • 故事讲完了，但没有回答题目里的每一个问题（为什么难忘、学到什么）。官方样本里 5 级的问题正是只讲了「做了什么」。The story is told but not every part of the prompt is answered (why it was memorable, what you learned). The official Level 5 sample did exactly that: what happened, not why.
  • 情绪词只有 happy、sad、nervous 三个。The only feeling words are happy, sad and nervous.
  • 每句都用 and then 接，听起来像流水账。Every sentence joined with "and then", so it sounds like a list.

冲 9 级以上的三个动作Three moves to reach 9+

  1. 开口前选一个最有画面的细节，用一句话把它说具体。Before you speak, choose one vivid detail and say it precisely in one sentence.
  2. 准备两个讲故事的衔接语（to make matters worse、luckily、in the end），每次至少用一个。Keep two storytelling linkers ready (to make matters worse, luckily, in the end) and use at least one every time.
  3. 最后一段说「这件事改变了我什么」，而不是「那天很开心」。End with what the experience changed in you, not "it was a nice day".

拿第 1 题对照看Side by side: prompt 1

Talk about a time when you were late for something important.

Level 5–65–6 级 · 72 words72 词

A few months ago, I had a job interview at nine o'clock. I got up at seven.

But the bus didn't come. I waited for twenty minutes. Then I took a taxi, but there was a lot of traffic. I was fifteen minutes late.

I was very embarrassed. I said sorry to the manager. She was nice, and the interview was OK.

Now I always leave one hour early for important things.

Level 7–87–8 级 · 116 words116 词

A few months ago, I had a job interview downtown at nine o'clock. I got up at seven, and I thought I had lots of time.

But when I got to the bus stop, the bus didn't come. I waited for twenty minutes, and I started to panic. Finally, I took a taxi, but there was a lot of traffic. I arrived fifteen minutes late.

I was so embarrassed. I said sorry to the manager and explained what happened. Luckily, she was very kind and the interview went well.

After that day, I learned an important lesson. Now I always leave one hour early for important things, and I check the bus schedule the night before.

Level 9+9 级以上 · 127 words127 词

A few months ago, I had a job interview downtown at nine, and I left home at seven, thinking I had plenty of time to spare.

Then everything went wrong. The bus simply never showed up, and after twenty minutes of pacing at the stop, I started to panic. I eventually flagged down a taxi, only to get stuck in rush-hour traffic, and I rushed in a good fifteen minutes late.

I was absolutely mortified. I apologized to the manager and briefly explained what had happened. Fortunately, she was very understanding, and the interview actually went well.

That morning taught me a lesson I've never forgotten. These days, I leave a full hour early for anything important, and I always check the bus schedule the night before.

Level 5–6 → 7–8从 5–6 级到 7–8 级

  1. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 I got up at seven. → I got up at seven, and I thought I had lots of time.
    加上当时的想法（以为时间很多），为后面的「出事」做铺垫，故事有起伏。Adding what you thought at the time (lots of time) sets up the problem, so the story has a turn.
  2. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 I waited for twenty minutes. → I waited for twenty minutes, and I started to panic.
    加上感受，听的人能跟着紧张起来。Adding the feeling lets the listener share the tension.

Level 7–8 → 9+从 7–8 级到 9 级以上

  1. Vocabulary词汇 I was so embarrassed. → I was absolutely mortified.
    mortified（羞愧极了）比 so embarrassed 更精准、更有分量。"Mortified" is more precise and stronger than "so embarrassed".
  2. Listenability好懂程度 Finally, I took a taxi, but there was a lot of traffic. → I eventually flagged down a taxi, only to get stuck in rush-hour traffic
    only to … 表示「结果却……」，是讲故事时制造反转的高级句型。"Only to ..." shows a disappointing result: an advanced pattern for a twist in a story.
  3. Listenability好懂程度 After that day, I learned an important lesson. → That morning taught me a lesson I've never forgotten.
    让 that morning 做主语，句式有变化，也比 learned a lesson 更有力。Making "that morning" the subject varies the sentence and is stronger than "I learned a lesson".
