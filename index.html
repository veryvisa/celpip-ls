你的目标Your target

入籍要求听力和口语各达到 CLB 4。CELPIP 级别 n 就等于 CLB n，所以法定下限是 4 级。建议两项都争取 5–6 级，给自己留安全边际，因为考场上总会有一两题不顺。Citizenship needs CLB 4 in listening and in speaking. A CELPIP level n equals CLB n, so 4 is the legal minimum. Aim for 5–6 in both skills, a safety margin for the one or two questions that go wrong on the day.口语是你进步最快的一项：它靠「模板 + 练习」，不靠词汇量。所以这个站先从口语开始；听力主要靠熟悉题型和记笔记。Speaking is the skill you can improve fastest, because it depends on templates and practice more than on vocabulary. That is why this site starts with speaking. Listening is mostly about knowing the question types and taking notes.

听力：每题答对得 1 分，答错不扣分，所以每题都要选；级别由电脑按得分和题目难度换算。官方学习包的对照（每次考试略有不同）：约 11–18 分≈4 级，17–23≈5 级，22–28≈6 级，27–31≈7 级。我们的经验法则：练习时稳定做对 24 题以上，就比较有把握到 5–6 级。Listening: each correct answer is 1 point, wrong answers are not penalised, so always answer. The computer converts points and question difficulty into a level. The official study pack shows approximate ranges (they overlap): about 11–18 points is level 4, 17–23 is 5, 22–28 is 6, 27–31 is 7. Our rule of thumb: if you score 24 or more in practice, level 5–6 is realistic.
