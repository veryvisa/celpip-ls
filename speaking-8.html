        官方怎么讲这个任务What the official packs say about this task

        看一幅不寻常的东西或情景的图，向一个看不到图的人描述（通常是打电话）。准备 30 秒，说 60 秒。Look at a picture of something unusual and describe it to someone who cannot see it (usually on the phone). 30 s to prepare, 60 s to speak.
        • 开头先进入角色：叫对方的名字，打招呼。Start by stepping into the role: greet the person by name.
        • 重点讲图里与众不同的地方。Describe what makes the picture unusual.
        • 挑你能讲好的部分讲。Focus on what you can describe well.
        • 想不出准确的词，就用常见词组合出意思。If the exact word will not come, build the meaning from common words.
        • 最后像真的打电话一样收尾挂电话。Finish the call properly at the end.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 直接叫了 Katia，说明打电话的目的；讲了一些场景细节（打鼓、用玻璃杯奏乐）。Addresses Katia and says why she is calling; some scene details (drumming, music from glasses).

        新朋友请你去他家，你到了那条街却找不到他的房子；打电话描述你站在哪里、问怎么走。A new friend invited you over; you are on his street but cannot find the house; call, say where you are standing and ask the way.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        我们的建议：怎么在本页练出来Our takeaways: how to practise it on this page

        这一部分是我们的建议，超出了官方学习包，不是官方内容。This part is our own advice, going beyond the official packs; it is not official content.
        • 模板：叫名字开场 → 它是什么 → 两三个不寻常的细节（位置 + 样子 + 声音或感受）→ 收尾挂电话。题目要你问对方什么（要不要来、怎么走），就在挂电话前问。Our template: open with the name, say what it is, give two or three unusual details (where, what it looks like, how it sounds or feels), end the call. If the task asks you to ask something (will they come, which way to go), ask it just before you hang up.
        • 缺词时用 “It’s like a …” “I don’t know how to say it, but …” “It’s about the size of …” 绕过去，这正是官方说的「用常见词组合意思」。短语库里都有。When a word is missing, go around it with “It’s like a …”, “I don’t know how to say it, but …” or “It’s about the size of …”, which is what the official advice means. All three are in the phrase bank.
        • 录完回放时听最后五秒：有没有道别（题目要求的话，还有那个问题）？没有就再录一次。On playback, listen to the last five seconds: is there a goodbye (and the question, if the task asks for one)? If not, record again.

        这个任务要你做什么What this task wants

        向一个看不到图的人描述图片里不寻常的东西或情景，像在打电话一样。Something odd is in the picture: describe it on the phone to a person who cannot see it.
        • 先「搭场」：Hi Jill, you won't believe what I'm looking at…Set up the role-play first.
        • 描述最特别的部分，说你能说得好的。Describe the unique parts you can describe well.
        • 缺词就用简单词拼：a big thing with wheels。结尾记得结束通话。Use common words if you lack a word; end the call at the close.

        官方时间：准备 30 秒，说话 60 秒。准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳转并发出提示音，录音自动开始。Official timing: 30 s prep and 60 s speaking. When prep ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear a prompt, and recording starts automatically.

        • 开头要「打电话」：Hi …, it's me。结尾要挂电话：Talk to you later。官方策略里都有。Start by "making the call": Hi ..., it's me. End by hanging up: Talk to you later. The official strategy has both.
        • 不确定的词不要卡住：用 a big round thing / something like a … 绕过去。Don't get stuck on words you're not sure of: go around them with "a big round thing" or "something like a ...".
        • 先说整体，再说最奇怪的部分，别平均用力。Say the overall picture first, then the strangest part. Don't put equal effort everywhere.

        答案模板Answer template

        1. 开场（打电话）1. Open the callHi Jill, it's me. You won't believe what I'm looking at right now!
        2. 整体：这是什么2. What is itIt looks like … but it is … It's the strangest …
        3. 最特别的细节3. The odd detailsOn top, … Next to it, … The weird part is …
        4. 收尾：结束通话4. End the callAnyway, I have to go. I'll send you a photo. Talk to you later!
