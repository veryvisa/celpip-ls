怎么用这三个级别How to use the three levels

  • 同一道题，我们写了三个级别的示范：5–6 级、7–8 级、9 级以上。内容骨架一样，差别只在四个评分维度上一级一级往上加。先找到和自己最像的那一栏，再看下一栏把哪几句换掉了。For every prompt we wrote three versions: Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+. The skeleton is the same; each level adds to the four rating dimensions. Find the column that sounds most like you, then see which sentences the next column changes.
  • 5–6 级示范是「干净版」：真实的 5–6 级录音里还会有停顿、重说和一些小语法错，我们没有故意写错，免得你把错的学进去。你自己的录音如果更短、更断，那正是这一级常见的样子。The Level 5–6 versions are clean: real Level 5–6 speech also has pauses, restarts and small grammar slips, but we did not write mistakes in, so you will not learn them. If your own recording is shorter and choppier, that is normal for this level.
  • 这些都是我们写的示范，不是官方样本，也没有经过官方评分员打分。级别是按官方四个维度和成绩单上 5 级、9 级的描述来写的。These are our own examples, not official samples, and no official rater has scored them. The levels follow the official four dimensions and the Level 5 and Level 9 score-report descriptions.

5–6 级长什么样What Level 5–6 looks like

  • 三条建议都有，但每条只有一两句，理由常常是 good、very bad 这样的笼统词。All three tips are there, but each gets one or two sentences, and reasons are vague words like "good" or "very bad".
  • 句子短，多用 and、but、so 连接；开头结尾有，但很简单。Short sentences joined by and, but, so; an opening and a closing, but plain.
  • 内容基本切题，对方是谁听得出来，但语气变化少。On topic, and you can tell who the listener is, but the tone hardly changes.

7–8 级长什么样What Level 7–8 looks like

  • 每条建议后面都有「为什么」或「我自己怎么做」，有一个小故事。Every tip has a why or a "what I do", and there is a short story.
  • 用词具体（icy、warning sign、impulse buy），有一两句可以直接说的话。Specific words (icy, warning sign, impulse buy) and a ready-made sentence or two.
  • 开头安慰、结尾鼓励，语气像朋友。Reassuring opening, encouraging close: it sounds like a friend.

9 级以上长什么样What Level 9+ looks like

  • 理由有深度：不只说做什么，还说出背后的原因或后果（不是冷，是湿冷磨人）。Reasons have depth: not just what to do but the cause or consequence behind it (it's the damp, not the cold).
  • 自然的习语和搭配（red flag、make a dent in、tap into your network），每段一两处，不堆砌。Natural idioms and collocations (red flag, make a dent in, tap into your network), one or two per paragraph, never piled up.
  • 句式多变：强调句、whereas 对比、插入语；三条建议之间有清楚的衔接。Varied sentences: clefts, whereas contrasts, asides; smooth links between the tips.

为什么常卡在 7 级Why people get stuck at 7

  • 三条建议是同一层次的（都是「买东西」），没有从不同角度给。The three tips are all the same kind (all "buy things") instead of coming from different angles.
  • 每条建议的理由一样长、一样平，没有一条说透。Every reason is equally short and flat; none is fully developed.
  • 用词正确但都是最常见的词（good、important、help），评分员听不到「精准」。The words are correct but always the most common ones (good, important, help), so the rater hears no precision.

冲 9 级以上的三个动作Three moves to reach 9+

  1. 挑一条建议说透：做什么→为什么→不做会怎样→我的经历。Develop one tip fully: what to do, why, what happens if you don't, your own experience.
  2. 每段换一个更精准的词或一个地道搭配，替换掉 good / very / a lot。In each paragraph swap one plain word (good, very, a lot) for a precise word or natural collocation.
  3. 结尾不要只说 good luck，用一句话总结「为什么这样做就够了」。Do not end with just "good luck": sum up in one line why these steps are enough.

拿第 1 题对照看Side by side: prompt 1

Your neighbour just arrived in BC and will face her first winter. She asks what she should do to get ready. Give her advice.

Level 5–65–6 级 · 121 words121 词

Hi Maria, don't worry. Winter in BC is OK if you get ready.

First, buy a warm coat and good boots. It rains a lot here. Wet feet are very bad. I bought my boots in October.

Second, check the weather every morning. When it snows, the roads are dangerous and the bus is late. I use an app on my phone, so I know what to wear.

Third, if you have a car, put an emergency kit in it. Put a blanket, a flashlight, water and snacks. Last winter my car was stuck in the snow, and the blanket helped me a lot.

So that's my advice. It's not hard. You will be fine. Call me if you need help!

Level 7–87–8 级 · 157 words157 词

Hi Maria, don't worry. Winter in BC is not so bad if you get ready.

First, buy a warm coat and a pair of waterproof boots. It rains a lot here, and wet feet are really uncomfortable. I bought my boots in October, and they were on sale.

Second, check the weather and the road conditions every morning. When it snows, the roads can be icy, and the bus can be late. I use a weather app on my phone, so I always know what to wear.

Finally, if you have a car, keep a small emergency kit in it. Put in a blanket, a flashlight, some water and snacks. You probably won't need it, but it makes you feel safe. Last winter my car got stuck in the snow, and the blanket really helped me.

So that's my advice. It's not hard, and you will be fine. Stay warm, and call me if you need anything!

Level 9+9 级以上 · 191 words191 词

Hi Maria, welcome to your first BC winter! Honestly, it's far milder than people expect, as long as you prepare a little.

First and foremost, invest in a warm, waterproof coat and a pair of insulated boots. Here on the coast it's the rain, not the cold, that wears you down, and nothing ruins a day faster than soaking-wet feet. I picked up my boots in October, when they were on sale, which saved me a fair bit.

Second, make a habit of checking the forecast and the road conditions before you head out. Even a light dusting of snow can turn the roads icy, and buses tend to run behind schedule. A good weather app takes the guesswork out of what to wear.

Finally, if you drive, keep a small emergency kit in the trunk: a blanket, a flashlight, water and a few snacks. Chances are you'll never need it, but it's peace of mind. Last January I was stuck in a snowbank for an hour, and that blanket was a lifesaver.

All in all, it's very manageable. Stay warm, and don't hesitate to call me if anything comes up!

Level 5–6 → 7–8从 5–6 级到 7–8 级

  1. Vocabulary词汇 Wet feet are very bad. → wet feet are really uncomfortable
    very bad 太笼统；really uncomfortable 说清了「坏在哪」，还用 and 把两句连成一句。"Very bad" is vague; "really uncomfortable" says what is bad, and "and" joins the two short sentences.
  2. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 the roads are dangerous and the bus is late → the roads can be icy, and the bus can be late
    can be 表示「有可能」，比一口咬定的 are 更准确；icy 比 dangerous 具体。"Can be" says it may happen, which is more accurate than "are"; "icy" is more specific than "dangerous".

Level 7–8 → 9+从 7–8 级到 9 级以上

  1. Vocabulary词汇 Winter in BC is not so bad if you get ready. → it's far milder than people expect, as long as you prepare a little
    far milder than people expect 是比较级加对比，as long as 是条件连接词，一句话里同时安慰又给条件。"Far milder than people expect" uses a comparison, and "as long as" adds a condition: reassurance and advice in one sentence.
  2. Listenability好懂程度 It rains a lot here, and wet feet are really uncomfortable. → it's the rain, not the cold, that wears you down
    it's … that 强调句把重点放在「雨而不是冷」，这是 9 级描述里的「复杂句型」。The "it's ... that" cleft puts the focus on rain, not cold: the kind of complex structure the Level 9 description mentions.
  3. Vocabulary词汇 You probably won't need it, but it makes you feel safe. → Chances are you'll never need it, but it's peace of mind.
    chances are、peace of mind 是母语者常用的固定说法，自然不生硬。"Chances are" and "peace of mind" are everyday native expressions that sound natural, not forced.
