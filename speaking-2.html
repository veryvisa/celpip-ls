        官方怎么讲这个任务What the official packs say about this task

        讲一件过去发生过的事。准备 30 秒，说 60 秒。Tell a story about something that happened in the past. 30 s to prepare, 60 s to speak.
        • 很快定下讲哪件事。Decide quickly which story to tell.
        • 想不起真事，就编一个。If no real story comes to mind, invent one.
        • 用过去时。Use past tenses.
        • 加上描述性的细节。Add descriptive details.
        • 让听的人感受到你当时的心情。Help the listener feel what you felt.

        Official level 5官方评分 5 级 Speaking Pro Target 5 study pack, p. 11官方 Target 5 学习包第 11 页
        讲一门你很喜欢的课。Talk about a class you really enjoyed.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 说出了课上具体做的事（设计问卷、发问卷、收集、总结）。Names concrete things done in the class (making a survey, handing it out, collecting and summing up answers).
        • 用了 first of all 这样的顺序词；发音大多清楚。Uses a sequence word like “first of all”; pronunciation is mostly clear.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 一直没说为什么喜欢这门课，题目的一部分没回答。Never says why she enjoyed the class, so part of the question is left unanswered.
        • 反复重说、半句重来；句子简单，小语法错误很多；想法没展开。Many repeats and restarts; simple sentences with frequent small errors; ideas not developed.
        Official level 8官方评分 8 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 12官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 12 页
        讲一次你教别人做某件事的经历（这里是教朋友滑雪）。Talk about a time you taught someone something (here: teaching a friend to ski).

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 讲清了教学的主要步骤，用了滑雪相关的专门词。Covers the main steps of the lesson with skiing-specific words.
        • 发音清楚；题目各部分都答到了。Clear pronunciation; every part of the question is addressed.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 一直用 so 接下一句，句式单调；有几处动词搭配错误。Every sentence starts with “so”; a few wrong verb patterns.
        • 点评明说：要到 9 级以上，需要更多展开和更具体的细节。The analysis says outright: more development and more specific detail are needed for 9+.
        Official level 12官方评分 12 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 13官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 13 页
        同一道「教别人」的题（教朋友准备一顿节日晚餐）。Same “teach someone” question (helping a friend cook a holiday dinner).

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 先交代事情，再按顺序讲清每一步（定菜单、买菜、提前试做），时间词一个接一个。States the situation, then walks through each step in order (menu, shopping, trial runs) with a chain of time words.
        • 细节精确（年龄、节日、传统菜单、写好的步骤）；用了多种时态，包括过去完成时；语调自然。Precise details (ages, the holiday, a traditional menu, written steps); a range of tenses including the past perfect; natural intonation.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 有一两个含糊的口头说法，一处半句重来。One or two vague fillers and one restart.
        • 最后被时间截断，但该讲的部分在截断前都讲完了，所以不影响。Cut off by the timer, but every part was covered before that, so it does not hurt.
        Official level 9官方评分 9 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 14官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 14 页
        和上面 5 级样本同一道题：喜欢的课。Same question as the Level 5 sample: a class you enjoyed.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 给了几个喜欢的理由，并用一件小事展开；有开头也有收尾。Gives several reasons it was enjoyable and develops one with a small anecdote; has an opening and a closing.
        • 语调带出了感情；用了不太常见的短语和较复杂的句子。Intonation carries feeling; less common phrases and some complex sentences.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 一个习语用错了，不过马上自己改正了。Mixes up one idiom, then corrects it.

        高分样本多做了什么What the higher-level samples did differently

        • 同一道「喜欢的课」：5 级讲了课上做了什么，却没讲为什么喜欢；9 级回答了「为什么」，还用一件小事让人感受到她的心情。题目里的每个问号都要有回答。Same “class” question: Level 5 says what the class did but never why she liked it; Level 9 answers the why and uses a small story so you feel it. Every question in the prompt needs an answer.
        • 12 级的样本说明：清楚的时间顺序加上精确的小细节，比华丽的词更值钱。The Level 12 sample shows that a clear time order plus small precise details is worth more than fancy words.

        这个任务要你做什么What this task wants

        讲一个过去的经历故事。想不出真事，就编一个。Tell a story about something that happened to you in the past.
        • 30 秒内马上定一个故事，不要换来换去。Decide on one story fast and stay with it.
        • 想不出真事就现编，评分看的是英语，不查事实。If you cannot think of a real one, make one up.
        • 用过去时，加细节（时间、地点、人、感受）。Past tense, descriptive details, and how you felt.

        官方时间：准备 30 秒，说话 60 秒。准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳转并发出提示音，录音自动开始。Official timing: 30 s prep and 60 s speaking. When prep ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear a prompt, and recording starts automatically.

        • 故事不用惊天动地，普通小事讲清楚就行。The story doesn't need to be dramatic. An ordinary small event told clearly is enough.
        • 全程过去时：was / went / told。现在时混进来会扣听感分。Use the past tense throughout: was / went / told. Mixing in the present tense loses points on how it sounds.
        • 时间还剩就加一个感受或一个小细节，别沉默。If you have time left, add a feeling or a small detail. Don't stay silent.

        答案模板Answer template

        1. 开头：时间 + 地点 + 谁1. Set the sceneA few months ago, I was … when …
        2. 发生了什么（2–3 个动作）2. What happenedFirst, … Then, … After that, …
        3. 结果 / 感受3. Result and feelingIn the end, … I felt really …
        4. 一句收尾4. Wrap upIt was a great experience, and I learned that …
