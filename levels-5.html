怎么用这三个级别How to use the three levels

  • 同一道题，我们写了三个级别的示范：5–6 级、7–8 级、9 级以上。内容骨架一样，差别只在四个评分维度上一级一级往上加。先找到和自己最像的那一栏，再看下一栏把哪几句换掉了。For every prompt we wrote three versions: Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+. The skeleton is the same; each level adds to the four rating dimensions. Find the column that sounds most like you, then see which sentences the next column changes.
  • 5–6 级示范是「干净版」：真实的 5–6 级录音里还会有停顿、重说和一些小语法错，我们没有故意写错，免得你把错的学进去。你自己的录音如果更短、更断，那正是这一级常见的样子。The Level 5–6 versions are clean: real Level 5–6 speech also has pauses, restarts and small grammar slips, but we did not write mistakes in, so you will not learn them. If your own recording is shorter and choppier, that is normal for this level.
  • 这些都是我们写的示范，不是官方样本，也没有经过官方评分员打分。级别是按官方四个维度和成绩单上 5 级、9 级的描述来写的。These are our own examples, not official samples, and no official rater has scored them. The levels follow the official four dimensions and the Level 5 and Level 9 score-report descriptions.

What Level 9+ adds is density, not speed.

5–6 级长什么样What Level 5–6 looks like

  • 把卡片上的信息念一遍（有车位、两室），没说这些对对方有什么用。官方 5 级样本就是这样。Reads the card back (parking, two bedrooms) without saying what it means for the listener, just like the official Level 5 sample.
  • 很少回应对方提出的新方案。Barely answers the other person's new suggestion.
  • 结尾 So I like Option B，语气不像在说服。Ends "So I like Option B", which does not sound persuasive.

7–8 级长什么样What Level 7–8 looks like

  • 先肯定对方方案的优点，再说自己为什么更好。Acknowledges the other option's strength, then says why yours is better.
  • 把卡片上的事实和对方的生活连起来（有车位→买菜方便）。Connects the card facts to the listener's life (parking means easy grocery runs).
  • 承认自己方案的缺点（贵、远），再说为什么值得。Admits your option's weakness (cost, distance) and says why it is worth it.

9 级以上长什么样What Level 9+ looks like

  • 加入卡片上没有的理由（孩子用东西不爱惜、照片拍不出花园的美），官方 12 级样本就是这样拉开差距的。Adds reasons that are not on the card (how kids treat devices, photos don't do the gardens justice); that is how the official Level 12 sample pulled ahead.
  • 用 whereas、on top of that、granted 等把比较和让步说清楚。Uses whereas, on top of that and granted to compare and concede clearly.
  • 说服的语气对准对象：对经理用商务词，对家人给折中方案、主动揽活。Aims the tone at the listener: business words for a manager, a compromise and an offer to help for family.

为什么常卡在 7 级Why people get stuck at 7

  • 只比较自己选的方案和原来的另一个选项，忘了对方提的新方案。Compares only your choice with the original other option and forgets the new suggestion.
  • 理由全部来自卡片，没有自己的判断。Every reason comes from the card; none is your own judgment.
  • 没有让步，听起来像在争吵而不是说服。No concession, so it sounds like arguing, not persuading.

冲 9 级以上的三个动作Three moves to reach 9+

  1. 第一句先说对方方案的一个真优点。In your first sentence, give one real strength of their idea.
  2. 准备一个卡片上没有的理由，最好和这个人的处境有关。Prepare one reason that is not on the card, ideally about this person's situation.
  3. 用一句「虽然……但是……」处理自己方案的缺点。Handle your option's weakness in one "although ..., ..." sentence.

拿第 1 题对照看Side by side: prompt 1

Your friend is choosing a place to live. Option A: a one-bedroom apartment near the SkyTrain, $2,000 per month, small, no parking. Option B: a two-bedroom townhouse in a quiet area, $2,400 per month, 25 minutes from the station, with parking. You chose Option B. Your friend suggests renting a room in a shared house instead: $900 per month, shared kitchen and bathroom, 10 minutes' walk to the SkyTrain, no parking. Persuade him that Option B is better.

Level 5–65–6 级 · 69 words69 词

Thanks for the idea. The room is cheap. But I think Option B is better.

First, the townhouse has two bedrooms. In the shared house, you share the kitchen and the bathroom. It is not good.

Second, the townhouse has parking. The shared house has no parking.

The townhouse is more money, but it is better for you. You can have your own room. So I like Option B.

Level 7–87–8 级 · 121 words121 词

Thanks for the idea. A room for nine hundred dollars is cheap, and it's close to the SkyTrain. But I still think Option B is better.

First, the townhouse has two bedrooms, so you will have your own space. In a shared house, you must share the kitchen and the bathroom, and that can be stressful when you come home tired.

Second, the townhouse has parking. You can drive to the grocery store and carry everything home easily. In the shared house, you have no parking, and it's hard to shop without a car.

Yes, it's twenty-five minutes from the station, and it costs fifteen hundred more. But I think privacy and comfort are worth it. So I recommend Option B.

Level 9+9 级以上 · 135 words135 词

I'll admit nine hundred a month for a room right by the SkyTrain is hard to beat on price. Even so, I'm convinced the townhouse is the smarter move for you.

For one thing, you'd have a whole second bedroom, which means real privacy. In a shared house you'd be juggling the kitchen and bathroom with strangers, and after a long day that wears thin very quickly.

What's more, the townhouse comes with parking. You could do one big grocery run a week and unload right at your door, whereas in the shared house you'd be hauling bags on foot.

Granted, it's twenty-five minutes from the station and fifteen hundred more a month. But for the peace and comfort you'd get, I honestly think it's money well spent. That's why I'd go with Option B.

Level 5–6 → 7–8从 5–6 级到 7–8 级

  1. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 It is not good. → that can be stressful when you come home tired
    说出合住到底哪里不好（下班累了还要抢厨房），理由才站得住。Saying exactly what is bad (sharing when you come home tired) makes the reason stand.
  2. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 The shared house has no parking. → You can drive to the grocery store and carry everything home easily.
    5 级只把卡片念一遍（有车位、没车位）；7 级说出车位对他生活的用处。Level 5 reads the card back (parking, no parking); Level 7 says what parking does for his life.

Level 7–8 → 9+从 7–8 级到 9 级以上

  1. Vocabulary词汇 A room for nine hundred dollars is cheap, and it's close to the SkyTrain. → nine hundred a month for a room right by the SkyTrain is hard to beat on price
    hard to beat on price 承认对方优点又不夸张，让步更真诚。"Hard to beat on price" concedes the other side's strength without overdoing it.
  2. Listenability好懂程度 In the shared house, you have no parking, and it's hard to shop without a car. → whereas in the shared house you'd be hauling bags on foot
    whereas 直接对比两个选项，这正是官方说的「比较」。"Whereas" compares the two options directly, which is what the task asks for.
  3. Vocabulary词汇 But I think privacy and comfort are worth it. → for the peace and comfort you'd get, I honestly think it's money well spent
    money well spent（钱花得值）是说服时的地道说法。"Money well spent" is the natural phrase for persuading about cost.
