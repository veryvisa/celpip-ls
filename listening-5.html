常见陷阱Common question traps

  • 给三个说话人标记Label three speakers
    他们出场时，按画面位置（左、中、右）标好，再各记一条观点。Write their positions on screen (left, middle, right) when they are introduced. Then note one opinion for each.
  • 谁同意谁Who agrees with whom
    题目会问两个人同意什么、谁不同意。用箭头记：A→B（同意）、A≠C。Questions ask what two speakers agree about, or who disagrees. Use arrows: A → B (agree) / A ≠ C.
  • 观点会变Opinion changes
    有人一开始反对，后来被说服。用「→」记下变化。A speaker may start against an idea and finish for it. Mark changes with “→”.
  • 控制节奏Pacing
    每题大约 30 秒。难题先跳过，回头再做；可以任意顺序。About 30 seconds per question. Skip a hard one and return; you can answer in any order.

记笔记方法Note-taking tips

画三个小框代表三个说话人，每框写观点；用箭头标谁同意谁。Draw three small boxes for the three speakers and write each opinion; use arrows for agreement.
  • 用要点和缩写，不写整句；你的笔记没人看，不影响分数。Use short points and abbreviations. Nobody reads your notes and they do not affect your score.
  • 按顺序记：题目的顺序大致和答案在音频里出现的顺序一致。Take notes in order. Questions come in roughly the same order as the answers in the audio.
  • 考前用纸和笔练（敲键盘不是同一种体验），找到你自己的缩写系统。Practise with pen and paper before the test, and build your own abbreviation system.
  • 考场发纸和笔；需要更多纸可以举手要。You get paper and a pen at the test centre; raise your hand for more paper.
