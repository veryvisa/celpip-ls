常见陷阱Common question traps

  • 谁负责什么Who does what
    计划会分给两个人。笔记写成「A：……、B：……」，免得混淆。Plans are split between two people. Write A: ... and B: ... so you do not swap them.
  • 时间、地点和更改Time, place, and changes
    计划会变。记一个小日历：日期、时间、地点，旧的划掉。Plans move. Keep a mini calendar: day, time, place, and cross out old ones.
  • 同意还是只是建议Agreement vs. suggestion
    「可能行」不是同意；「好，就这么办」才是。听最后的确认。“That might work” is not a yes. “Sure, let's do it” is. Listen for the final agreement.
  • 口语缩读Casual speech
    gonna、wanna、d'you 这类缩读要练习去「听」，不能只认书面形式。gonna, wanna, I'll have to, d'you. Practise hearing reduced sounds, not just the written forms.

记笔记方法Note-taking tips

两列：A 做什么 / B 做什么；底下一行写时间、地点和最后确认。Two columns: what A does / what B does; one line under them for time, place and final agreement.
  • 用要点和缩写，不写整句；你的笔记没人看，不影响分数。Use short points and abbreviations. Nobody reads your notes and they do not affect your score.
  • 按顺序记：题目的顺序大致和答案在音频里出现的顺序一致。Take notes in order. Questions come in roughly the same order as the answers in the audio.
  • 考前用纸和笔练（敲键盘不是同一种体验），找到你自己的缩写系统。Practise with pen and paper before the test, and build your own abbreviation system.
  • 考场发纸和笔；需要更多纸可以举手要。You get paper and a pen at the test centre; raise your hand for more paper.
