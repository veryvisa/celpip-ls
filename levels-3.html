怎么用这三个级别How to use the three levels

  • 同一道题，我们写了三个级别的示范：5–6 级、7–8 级、9 级以上。内容骨架一样，差别只在四个评分维度上一级一级往上加。先找到和自己最像的那一栏，再看下一栏把哪几句换掉了。For every prompt we wrote three versions: Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+. The skeleton is the same; each level adds to the four rating dimensions. Find the column that sounds most like you, then see which sentences the next column changes.
  • 5–6 级示范是「干净版」：真实的 5–6 级录音里还会有停顿、重说和一些小语法错，我们没有故意写错，免得你把错的学进去。你自己的录音如果更短、更断，那正是这一级常见的样子。The Level 5–6 versions are clean: real Level 5–6 speech also has pauses, restarts and small grammar slips, but we did not write mistakes in, so you will not learn them. If your own recording is shorter and choppier, that is normal for this level.
  • 这些都是我们写的示范，不是官方样本，也没有经过官方评分员打分。级别是按官方四个维度和成绩单上 5 级、9 级的描述来写的。These are our own examples, not official samples, and no official rater has scored them. The levels follow the official four dimensions and the Level 5 and Level 9 score-report descriptions.

5–6 级长什么样What Level 5–6 looks like

  • 一个个列出人和物（There is…, A man is…），听者知道有什么，但很难想象位置和气氛。Lists people and things (There is..., A man is...); the listener knows what is there but cannot picture where or the mood.
  • 很少用方位词，几乎没有推测。Few location words, almost no guessing.
  • 结尾一句 It is nice。Ends with "It is nice".

7–8 级长什么样What Level 7–8 looks like

  • 先交代场景，再用 In the front / Next to her / In the background 带着听者看。Sets the scene, then guides the listener: in the front, next to her, in the background.
  • 每个人物有动作＋表情，偶尔推测（probably、maybe）。Each person has an action and an expression, with some guesses (probably, maybe).
  • 结尾概括全图气氛。Ends by summing up the mood.

9 级以上长什么样What Level 9+ looks like

  • 动作用精准动词（scramble、sample、clutch），用一个细节代替形容词（鼻子贴着玻璃＝很想要）。Precise verbs (scramble, sample, clutch) and a detail instead of an adjective (nose pressed to the glass = wanting).
  • 用 while、who、which 从句把两个人或细节连成一句。While, who and which clauses join two people or details in one sentence.
  • 点出全图的「焦点」或「紧张点」，像官方 9 级样本那样先数、先定位，再描述。Names the focus or point of tension, and, like the official Level 9 sample, counts and locates before describing.

为什么常卡在 7 级Why people get stuck at 7

  • 想描述所有东西，每样只说半句，结果哪样都不清楚。Tries to mention everything, half a sentence each, so nothing is clear.
  • 只说看到的，不说「这说明什么」。Only says what is seen, never what it suggests.
  • 一直用 is + -ing，句型没有变化。Always "is + -ing"; no variety.

冲 9 级以上的三个动作Three moves to reach 9+

  1. 准备的 30 秒里选 4 个重点，按位置排好顺序。In the 30 seconds of prep, pick four focus points and order them by location.
  2. 每个人物说「动作＋一个细节＋一个推测」。For each person give action, one detail, one guess.
  3. 至少用一次 while 或 who 把两个画面连起来。Use while or who at least once to join two parts of the picture.

拿第 1 题对照看Side by side: prompt 1

A farmers' market on a sunny Saturday morning. A woman is buying apples from a smiling seller. An older man holds a cloth bag and tastes a piece of cheese. A girl holds a balloon. A dog waits next to a table of flowers. Colourful tents and a few musicians in the background.

Level 5–65–6 级 · 72 words72 词

This picture is a farmers' market. It is sunny. A woman is buying apples. The seller is smiling.

An old man has a bag. He is eating cheese.

There is a girl with a balloon. She is happy. There is a dog next to the flowers.

In the back, there are tents. They have many colours. Some people are playing music. It is a nice place. I think people like this market.

Level 7–87–8 级 · 121 words121 词

This picture shows a farmers' market on a sunny Saturday morning. In the front, a woman is buying apples from a seller. The seller is smiling, so they probably know each other.

Next to her, an older man is holding a cloth bag. He is trying a piece of cheese, and he looks like he likes it.

On the right, a little girl is holding a balloon. She seems really happy. Next to a table of flowers, a dog is waiting quietly. Maybe he is waiting for his owner.

In the background, I can see colourful tents, and a few musicians are playing music. Overall, it looks like a relaxed and happy place. I think people enjoy coming here on weekends.

Level 9+9 级以上 · 135 words135 词

This picture shows a farmers' market on a bright Saturday morning. Right in the foreground, a woman is picking out apples from a cheerful vendor; judging by their smiles, they might well be regulars who know each other.

Just beside her, an older gentleman with a cloth bag over his arm is sampling a piece of cheese, and he looks rather impressed.

Off to the right, a little girl is clutching a balloon, beaming from ear to ear, while a dog waits patiently next to a table piled high with flowers, presumably for its owner to finish shopping.

In the background, there's a row of colourful tents and a few musicians playing for the crowd. All in all, it's a relaxed, lively scene, the kind of place people love to wander through on a weekend.

Level 5–6 → 7–8从 5–6 级到 7–8 级

  1. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 A woman is buying apples. The seller is smiling. → a woman is buying apples from a seller. The seller is smiling, so they probably know each other.
    看到微笑后再推一步（可能认识），描述就有了「解读」。Going one step past the smile (they probably know each other) adds interpretation.
  2. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 This picture is a farmers' market. It is sunny. → This picture shows a farmers' market on a sunny Saturday morning. In the front
    开头一句交代地点和时间，再用 In the front 标位置，听者能跟上。One opening sentence gives place and time, then "In the front" marks location so the listener can follow.

Level 7–8 → 9+从 7–8 级到 9 级以上

  1. Vocabulary词汇 He is trying a piece of cheese, and he looks like he likes it. → is sampling a piece of cheese, and he looks rather impressed
    sample（试吃）、rather impressed 比 trying、likes it 更精准。"Sampling" and "rather impressed" are more precise than "trying" and "likes it".
  2. Vocabulary词汇 She seems really happy. → beaming from ear to ear
    beam from ear to ear（笑得合不拢嘴）是生动的固定说法。"Beaming from ear to ear" is a vivid set phrase.
  3. Listenability好懂程度 Next to a table of flowers, a dog is waiting quietly. Maybe he is waiting for his owner. → while a dog waits patiently next to a table piled high with flowers, presumably for its owner to finish shopping
    while 把两个人物连成一句，piled high with、presumably 让细节和推测都更精确。"While" links two subjects in one sentence; "piled high with" and "presumably" sharpen the detail and the guess.
