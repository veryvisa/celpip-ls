        Official level 5官方评分 5 级 Speaking Pro Target 5 study pack, p. 15官方 Target 5 学习包第 15 页
        同一家超市，接下来会怎样。The same supermarket: what happens next.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 做了几个预测（离开、付钱、继续装购物车）；指认人物时用了合适的短语。Makes several predictions (leave, pay, keep filling the cart); some good phrases to point out people.
        • 发音大多清楚，节奏稳但偏慢。Mostly clear pronunciation; steady but slow rhythm.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 只用 and 连接，反复说 I think；用词需要更准、更多样（比如称呼人物太口语）。Only and as a link, I think over and over; words need more precision and variety (some childish words for people).
        • 停顿和打断让内容不完整。Pauses and interruptions leave the answer incomplete.
        Official level 8官方评分 8 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 19官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 19 页
        电影院大厅，接下来会怎样。The cinema lobby: what happens next.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 预测都切题；常见词用得准。Relevant predictions; common words used accurately.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 预测少，也没用例子展开；同一个预测说了两遍。Few predictions, not developed with examples; one prediction is repeated.
        • 停顿和嗯啊打断节奏；多为简单句；过渡词要更有效。Pauses and fillers break the rhythm; mostly simple sentences; transitions need work.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 预测多，而且每个都先讲清是图里哪一个人（穿什么、在哪）。Many predictions, and each one first says exactly which person it is about (what they wear, where they are).
        • 用了跟场景相关的词（扫码、走出店门等）；开头有效；开始出现较复杂的句子。Situation words (scanning, stepping out and so on); an effective opening; some complex grammar emerging.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 语速有时太快，听着吃力；个别用词不准。Sometimes too fast to follow; a few imprecise words.
        • 结尾可以更好。The closing could be better.

        高分样本多做了什么What the higher-level samples did differently

        • 同一幅图：5 级的预测指向模糊（「那个男孩」「另一个」）；9 级先把人说清楚（「穿蓝衣服的那个男孩」），再用 will probably / would 预测，还猜他的父母会怎么回应。Same picture: Level 5 points vaguely (“the boy”, “the other one”); Level 9 identifies the person first (“the boy in the blue shirt”), predicts with will probably / would, and even guesses how the parent will react.
        • 任务 4 的高分来自「动作 + 结果 + 感受」三连，而不是预测的数量。High marks in Task 4 come from action, result and feeling together, not from the number of predictions.

        这个任务要你做什么What this task wants

        还是刚才 Task 3 的那幅图，这次说「接下来可能发生什么」。想象要合理，要和图里的人在做的事相关。Look at the same illustration as in Task 3 and say what you think will happen next.
        • 预测要现实，和图里人物正在做的事有关。Be imaginative but realistic.
        • 想两件事：他们接下来会做什么？会有什么感受？What will they do, and how will they feel?
        • 多用 will / is going to / probably / might。Use future forms and probability words.

        官方时间：准备 30 秒，说话 60 秒。准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳转并发出提示音，录音自动开始。Official timing: 30 s prep and 60 s speaking. When prep ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear a prompt, and recording starts automatically.

        • 预测要和图里人物正在做的事有关，别突然跳到别的故事。Predictions should relate to what the people in the picture are doing. Don't jump to another story.
        • 每个预测配「为什么」或「感受」：because … / he will feel …。Give each prediction a "why" or a "feeling": because ... / he will feel ...
        • 时态用 will / is going to，别混成过去时。For tense, use will / is going to. Don't mix in the past tense.
