官方建议的练法，以及在本站怎么做How the packs suggest you practise, and how to do it here

  1. 官方：拿学习包里的样本，按四个维度逐项想：哪里可以更好？然后照着点评把它改写得更强。Official: Take the sample answers and ask, dimension by dimension, how each could be better; then rewrite them using the analysis as your guide.在本站：每个任务页的「官方样本」一节已经把 5 级和 9 级样本的强弱点列好了；改写时直接用本页的示范回答对照。On this site: Each task page now lists what the Level 5 and Level 9 samples did well and badly; compare with that page's worked example while you rewrite.
  2. 官方：讲座里的每道练习题都自己录一遍。Official: Record your own answer to every practice question from the webinar.在本站：本站每个任务 8–10 道原创题，都带计时和录音。On this site: Every task page has 8–10 original prompts with a timer and a recorder.
  3. 官方：每录一遍，对照评分标准表，想清楚哪里做得好、哪里要改。Official: After each recording, check it against the performance standards: what went well, what to fix.在本站：录完后的自查清单就是官方的四个维度。On this site: The self-check after each recording is the four official dimensions.
  4. 官方：把一段录音写成文字，花 20–30 分钟修改：改语法、加过渡词、换更多样的词、补具体细节、加几个复杂句。考试时不能这样改，但这能在考前长本事。Official: Write out one of your recordings and spend 20–30 minutes improving it: fix the grammar, add transitions, vary the words, add specific details, build a few longer sentences. You cannot revise on test day, but it builds the skill.在本站：录音可以存到设备上，回放时边听边写下来。On this site: Save your recording to your device and type it out while you listen.
  5. 官方：有选择的任务（5、6、7）每个选项都录一遍：任务 5 两个方案各录一次，任务 6 两个对象各录一次，任务 7 先答「是」再答「否」。Official: For the tasks with a choice (5, 6, 7), record every option: both products in Task 5, both people in Task 6, and in Task 7 answer yes, reset, then answer no.在本站：同一道题点「同一题再录一次」，换另一个选项再说。On this site: Use “Record this prompt again” and take the other option.
  6. 官方：一开始给自己比 30–60 秒更长的准备时间想要点和细节，再一点点缩短到考试时间。Official: Start with more than the real 30–60 s of prep to find ideas and details, then cut it back step by step to the real time.在本站：先在题目卡上看题想好（不计时），再按「开始」走真实计时。On this site: Read the prompt card and think as long as you like first, then press Start for the real timing.

考试当天的口语部分Speaking on test day

  • 口语约 15 分钟，是整场考试的最后一部分。Speaking takes about 15 minutes and is the last part of the test.
  • 准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳到下一步，你会听到开始说话的提示，录音自动开始。When prep time ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear the cue to start speaking, and recording starts automatically.
  • 用平常的音量说话。太大声或太小声都会拉低「好懂程度」；考场有一点背景声不要紧，评分员能听清正常音量的回答。Speak at your usual volume. Too loud or too soft hurts Listenability; a bit of background noise in the room is fine, the raters can hear a normal voice clearly.

5 级和 9 级：成绩单上的能力描述Level 5 and Level 9: what the score report says

官方成绩单除了口语级别，还会附三样东西：每个维度 1–3 句「我能……」的级别描述、一段该级别的能力概述、以及「接下来该练什么」的清单。下面是 5 级和 9 级这三样的意思，用我们的话说。Besides your Speaking level, the official score report adds three things: one to three “I can…” statements per dimension, a short profile of a typical speaker at that level, and a list of things to work on. Here is what they say for Level 5 and Level 9, in our words.

5 级：能做到什么Level 5: what you can do

Content / Coherence内容与连贯能说出自己的感受和偏好；能描述熟悉的东西、场合和人；能讲一件自己经历过的简单事情。Say how you feel and what you prefer; describe everyday things, places and people you know; tell a short story from your own life.
Vocabulary词汇用日常常见的词和短语。Everyday words and phrases.
Listenability好懂程度简单的语法句型基本能掌握。Reasonable control of simple grammar.
Task Fulfillment完成任务能把一个熟悉话题的信息传达清楚。Get information across on a topic you know.
When场合面对面、线上、电话都行；场合正式或随意都行。In person, online or by phone; in casual or formal situations.

In short一句话概括

在多数熟悉的场合，能说清感受和偏好，能描述熟悉的人、物和经历；语法简单，用词常见。In most familiar situations you can get your feelings and preferences across and describe people, things and experiences you know, using simple grammar and common words.

Things to work on at this level这个级别接下来该练的

  • 给观点和感受配上理由。Back up opinions and feelings with reasons.
  • 把人、物、场合描述得更长、更细。Give longer, more detailed descriptions.
  • 把简单语法练稳。Get simple grammar under firm control.
  • 多掌握一些表达同一个意思的词。Learn more ways to say what you mean.
  • 减少停顿和犹豫。Pause and hesitate less.
  • 减少让人听不懂的语法、用词和发音错误。Cut the grammar, word and pronunciation errors that make you hard to understand.

9 级：能做到什么Level 9: what you can do

Content / Coherence内容与连贯能讲清信息，并用有一定深度的理由支撑观点。Present information and support ideas with reasons of some depth.
Vocabulary词汇能用常见词，也能用和具体场合相关的专门词；会用一些比喻和习语。Common words plus words specific to the situation; some idioms and figures of speech.
Listenability好懂程度复杂句型有一定掌握；节奏、发音和语调大体流畅，别人听得懂。Some control of complex grammar; mostly fluent rhythm, pronunciation and intonation, easy to understand.
Task Fulfillment完成任务能根据不同的听众和场合调整说话方式和语气，把意思传达给对方。Adjust style and tone for different listeners and situations, and get your ideas across.
When场合和同辈或上级、权威人士说话都行；面对面、线上、电话；正式或随意。With peers or people in authority; in person, online or by phone; formal or informal.

In short一句话概括

在熟悉和不太熟悉的场合，都能讲清复杂的细节、感受和态度；掌握一部分复杂语法，工作场合需要的精准词汇够用；语法、用词、发音的错误很少妨碍理解。In familiar and less familiar situations you can get complex details, feelings and attitudes across. You control some complex grammar and have enough precise, work-ready vocabulary; your errors rarely get in the way of meaning.

Things to work on at this level这个级别接下来该练的

  • 用更多种复杂句型。Use a wider variety of complex sentences.
  • 多掌握和具体场合相关的词。Build more situation-specific vocabulary.
  • 继续减少语法、用词和发音错误。Keep reducing grammar, word and pronunciation errors.
  • 语气和说话方式要配合场合和听众。Match tone and style to the situation and listener.

Target 5 和 Target 9+ 学习包有什么不同How the Target 5 and Target 9+ packs differ

下面是我们读完两份学习包后的整理和统计，不是官方原话。This is our reading of the two packs, with our own counts; it is not official wording.
  • 两份学习包的任务说明和官方策略是同一套，一字不差；不同的是样本。Target 5 每个任务配 1 个样本，全部被评为 5 级；Target 9+ 每个任务配 2–3 个样本，级别在 8 到 12 之间。Both packs give the same task descriptions and the same official strategies. What differs is the samples: Target 5 has one sample per task, all rated Level 5; Target 9+ has two or three per task, rated from 8 to 12.
  • 最有用的一点：Target 9+ 里有 7 个任务用的是和 Target 5 同一道题（学费、喜欢的课、超市那张图、买风扇、宠物店、第二语言、街头艺人）。所以你可以拿同一道题，直接比 5 级和 9 级的差别。每个任务页的「官方样本」一节就是这样排的。The most useful thing: for 7 of the 8 tasks, Target 9+ reuses the very question from Target 5 (tuition, a favourite class, the supermarket picture, buying a fan, the pet shop, a second language, the street performers). So you can compare Level 5 and Level 9 on the same question. Each task page's “official samples” section is laid out that way.
  • 5 级样本的点评几乎都在说同几件事：细节不够、想法没展开（8 个里有 7 个），嗯啊和说一半重来打断了流畅（8 个都有），语气不够有说服力或不够热情（4 个），以及用 and / so 串起来的简单句。The Level 5 analyses keep naming the same few things: not enough detail or development (7 of 8), fillers and restarts breaking the flow (all 8), a tone that is not persuasive or warm enough (4 of 8), and simple sentences strung together with and / so.
  • 8 级和 9 级以上的分水岭，在样本点评里反复出现的是：每个点都有具体细节（数字、名称、位置）；用从句和多种时态（because、if、when、although、whereas、had been）；跟场合相关的专门词；有像样的开头和结尾；从头到尾立场一致；对着题目指定的人说话。Between 8 and 9+, the analyses keep pointing to: concrete detail for every point (numbers, names, positions); clauses and a range of tenses (because, if, when, although, whereas, had been); words specific to the situation; a proper opening and closing; one consistent position from start to finish; and speaking to the person the task names.
  • 也看得到什么不重要：9 级和 12 级的样本里照样有嗯啊、自我纠正、被时间截断的结尾。只要该说的部分说完了，被截断不是大问题。You can also see what matters less: the Level 9 and 12 samples still have fillers, self-corrections and endings cut off by the timer. Being cut off is not a big problem if you have already covered every part of the task.

从 5 级到 6–7 级：我们的建议From Level 5 to 6–7: our advice

这一节是我们的建议，根据官方样本的点评归纳，不是官方内容。This section is our advice, drawn from the official sample analyses; it is not official content.
  1. 每个点都配一个具体细节。5 级样本点评里最常见的一句就是「细节不够」。规则很简单：每说一个点，马上跟一个数字、名字、时间或地点。 → 答案模板Give every point one concrete detail. “Needs more detail” is the most common line in the Level 5 analyses. The rule is simple: after every point, add a number, a name, a time or a place. → answer template
  2. 说错了不重来，往下接。8 个 5 级样本都因为嗯啊和说一半重来被扣分。宁可用一个简单的说法说完整，也不要半句话重说两遍。 → 卡壳急救短语Do not restart; carry on. All 8 Level 5 samples lost marks for fillers and restarts. Say it simply and finish the sentence rather than starting it twice. → rescue phrases
  3. 先定结构，再开口：开头一句、三个点、收尾一句。9 级以上样本常被夸「开头和结尾都好」；8 级样本常被扣「没有收尾」。 → 任务 7 模板Fix the shape before you speak: one opening line, three points, one closing line. The 9+ samples are often praised for opening and closing; the Level 8 ones are marked down for having no close. → Task 7 template
  4. 每个回答放一个从句：because、if、when、although 任选一个。不用多，一两个就能让「句式变化」这一项往上走。 → 任务 7 短语Put one clause in every answer: because, if, when or although. One or two are enough to lift sentence variety. → Task 7 phrases
  5. 语气跟着任务走：劝人就要有说服力，邀朋友就要热情，跟顾客说话要礼貌而坚定。5 级样本有一半在这一项被提醒。 → 任务 6Let the task set the tone: persuade when you persuade, sound excited when you invite a friend, be polite but firm with a customer. Half of the Level 5 samples were told to fix their tone. → Task 6
  6. 用模考检查整体。单题练顺以后，一口气做完 8 个任务，看哪两个任务最弱，就回去重点练那两个。 → 整套模考Check the whole picture with the mock. Once single tasks feel smooth, do all 8 in a row, find your two weakest tasks and go back to those. → full mock test
