任务 8 分级说明：5–6、7–8、9 级以上各长什么样Task 8 levels: what Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+ look like

8 prompts × 3 levels8 道题 × 3 个级别Speak 60 s说话 60 秒Task 8: Describing an Unusual Situation任务 8：描述不寻常的情景

怎么用这三个级别How to use the three levels

  • 同一道题，我们写了三个级别的示范：5–6 级、7–8 级、9 级以上。内容骨架一样，差别只在四个评分维度上一级一级往上加。先找到和自己最像的那一栏，再看下一栏把哪几句换掉了。For every prompt we wrote three versions: Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+. The skeleton is the same; each level adds to the four rating dimensions. Find the column that sounds most like you, then see which sentences the next column changes.
  • 5–6 级示范是「干净版」：真实的 5–6 级录音里还会有停顿、重说和一些小语法错，我们没有故意写错，免得你把错的学进去。你自己的录音如果更短、更断，那正是这一级常见的样子。The Level 5–6 versions are clean: real Level 5–6 speech also has pauses, restarts and small grammar slips, but we did not write mistakes in, so you will not learn them. If your own recording is shorter and choppier, that is normal for this level.
  • 这些都是我们写的示范，不是官方样本，也没有经过官方评分员打分。级别是按官方四个维度和成绩单上 5 级、9 级的描述来写的。These are our own examples, not official samples, and no official rater has scored them. The levels follow the official four dimensions and the Level 5 and Level 9 score-report descriptions.

What Level 9+ adds is density, not speed.

5–6 级长什么样What Level 5–6 looks like

  • 说了图里有什么（一个人、一个沙发），但对方看不到图，很难想象。Says what is there (a man, a sofa), but the friend cannot see it and cannot picture it.
  • 没有点出「怪」在哪里。官方 5 级样本只说了「有人在打鼓」。Does not say what is strange; the official Level 5 sample only said someone was drumming.
  • 很少用打电话的语气。Little phone-call tone.

7–8 级长什么样What Level 7–8 looks like

  • 像打电话：开头抓住朋友注意，结尾说要挂了。Sounds like a call: a hook at the start and a sign-off at the end.
  • 给出大小、颜色、位置等让对方能想象的细节。Gives size, colour and position so the friend can picture it.
  • 用 The strangest part is … 点出最怪的地方。Uses "The strangest part is ..." to point out the oddest thing.

9 级以上长什么样What Level 9+ looks like

  • 用比喻和对比描述（像游泳比赛看台上的家长、普通的星期二），对方能「看到」也能笑出来。Uses comparisons and contrast (like a proud parent at a swim meet, just another Tuesday) so the friend sees it and laughs.
  • 精准的动词和习语（cling on for dear life、you could hear a pin drop）。Precise verbs and idioms (cling on for dear life, you could hear a pin drop).
  • 讲出这件事为什么怪、难在哪里、旁人怎么反应，官方 9 级样本就是这样加分的。Explains why it is strange, what is hard about it and how people react, which is how the official Level 9 sample scored.

为什么常卡在 7 级Why people get stuck at 7

  • 像任务 3 一样从前到后描述，忘了重点是「不寻常」。Describes front to back as in Task 3 and forgets the point is what is unusual.
  • 忘了对方看不到图，指代不清（this、that）。Forgets the friend cannot see it, so this and that are unclear.
  • 没有打电话的开头和结尾。No phone-call opening or closing.

冲 9 级以上的三个动作Three moves to reach 9+

  1. 第一句就说出最怪的那一点。Say the strangest thing in your first sentence.
  2. 给一个比喻（like…、as if…）让对方能想象。Give one comparison (like..., as if...) so the friend can imagine it.
  3. 加一个旁人的反应和一个自己的猜测。Add one bystander's reaction and one guess of your own.

拿第 1 题对照看Side by side: prompt 1

A man rides a bicycle carrying a big green sofa tied on the back, on a rainy street. People stop to take photos.

Level 5–65–6 级 · 69 words69 词

Hi Jill, it's me. I see a very strange thing!

A man is riding a bike in the rain. He has a big green sofa on his bike. The sofa is very big.

The sofa is wet. The man is riding.

People are stopping on the street. They are taking photos. One woman is laughing a lot.

I have to go now. I will send you a picture. Bye!

Level 7–87–8 级 · 120 words120 词

Hi Jill, it's me. You won't believe what I'm looking at right now!

There's a man riding a bicycle on a rainy street, and he has a huge green sofa on the back. It's tied to the bike with a rope, and it's almost as big as the bike!

The sofa is getting wet, but he doesn't seem to care. He's looking straight ahead, and he's holding the handlebars with both hands.

The strangest part is the people. A few of them stopped on the sidewalk, and they're taking photos with their phones. One woman is laughing so hard.

Anyway, I have to go before the rain gets worse. I'll send you a picture. Talk to you later, Jill. Bye!

Level 9+9 级以上 · 136 words136 词

Hi Jill, it's me. You are not going to believe what I'm looking at right now!

There's a guy cycling down a rain-soaked street with an enormous green sofa strapped to the back of his bike. It's lashed on with a couple of ropes, and honestly, it's nearly as wide as the bike itself, so he's wobbling a little.

The sofa is getting absolutely drenched, but he couldn't care less. He's gripping the handlebars with both hands and staring straight ahead, completely focused, as if this were perfectly normal.

What makes it even funnier is the reaction. Several people have stopped dead on the sidewalk to film him, and one woman is laughing so hard she's practically doubled over.

Anyway, I'd better run before the rain gets worse. I'll send you a photo. Talk soon. Bye!

Level 5–6 → 7–8从 5–6 级到 7–8 级

  1. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 He has a big green sofa on his bike. The sofa is very big. → he has a huge green sofa on the back. It's tied to the bike with a rope, and it's almost as big as the bike!
    说清怎么固定的、和车比有多大，对方看不到也能想象。Saying how it is tied on and how big it is next to the bike lets a listener who cannot see it picture it.
  2. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 The sofa is wet. The man is riding. → The sofa is getting wet, but he doesn't seem to care.
    but he doesn't seem to care 点出了「怪」在哪里——人的反应。"But he doesn't seem to care" points to what is strange: his reaction.

Level 7–8 → 9+从 7–8 级到 9 级以上

  1. Vocabulary词汇 There's a man riding a bicycle on a rainy street → There's a guy cycling down a rain-soaked street
    cycling down、rain-soaked 比 riding a bicycle on a rainy street 更紧凑生动。"Cycling down" and "rain-soaked" are tighter and livelier.
  2. Vocabulary词汇 The sofa is getting wet, but he doesn't seem to care. → The sofa is getting absolutely drenched, but he couldn't care less.
    drenched（湿透）、couldn't care less（毫不在乎）是地道强调说法。"Drenched" and "couldn't care less" are idiomatic intensifiers.
  3. Listenability好懂程度 The strangest part is the people. → What makes it even funnier is the reaction.
    what 主语从句把重点放在句尾，引出下一个画面。The "what" clause puts the focus at the end and leads into the next part.
