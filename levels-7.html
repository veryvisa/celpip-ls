怎么用这三个级别How to use the three levels

  • 同一道题，我们写了三个级别的示范：5–6 级、7–8 级、9 级以上。内容骨架一样，差别只在四个评分维度上一级一级往上加。先找到和自己最像的那一栏，再看下一栏把哪几句换掉了。For every prompt we wrote three versions: Level 5–6, 7–8 and 9+. The skeleton is the same; each level adds to the four rating dimensions. Find the column that sounds most like you, then see which sentences the next column changes.
  • 5–6 级示范是「干净版」：真实的 5–6 级录音里还会有停顿、重说和一些小语法错，我们没有故意写错，免得你把错的学进去。你自己的录音如果更短、更断，那正是这一级常见的样子。The Level 5–6 versions are clean: real Level 5–6 speech also has pauses, restarts and small grammar slips, but we did not write mistakes in, so you will not learn them. If your own recording is shorter and choppier, that is normal for this level.
  • 这些都是我们写的示范，不是官方样本，也没有经过官方评分员打分。级别是按官方四个维度和成绩单上 5 级、9 级的描述来写的。These are our own examples, not official samples, and no official rater has scored them. The levels follow the official four dimensions and the Level 5 and Level 9 score-report descriptions.

5–6 级长什么样What Level 5–6 looks like

  • 立场清楚，有三个理由，但每个理由一两句，有些只是换个说法重复观点。A clear position and three reasons, but each is one or two sentences, and some just restate the opinion.
  • 例子少，常用 good、bad、very。Few examples; lots of good, bad, very.
  • 会提到反方，但只说 Some people don't like it。Mentions the other side only as "some people don't like it".

7–8 级长什么样What Level 7–8 looks like

  • 每个理由有解释或个人例子。Each reason has an explanation or personal example.
  • 用 First / Second / Third 和 Of course … but … 组织。Organized with First, Second, Third and Of course ... but ....
  • 词汇贴题（commuting、work-life balance、dress code）。Topic words (commuting, work-life balance, dress code).

9 级以上长什么样What Level 9+ looks like

  • 理由有深度：说出因果链（收费→让人停下来想→少用），或指出数字和后果。Reasons have depth: a cause-and-effect chain (a fee makes people stop and think, so fewer bags) or numbers and consequences.
  • 立场一开始就说精确（for most purchases, though not all），后面的让步不自相矛盾。官方 8 级样本就是因为立场前后矛盾上不去。States the position precisely from the start (for most purchases, though not all) so later concessions do not contradict it. The official Level 8 sample stalled because it contradicted itself.
  • 让步与反驳用成熟句型：I'll grant that … Even so、on balance、the benefits far outweigh the drawbacks。Mature concession and rebuttal: I'll grant that ... Even so, on balance, the benefits far outweigh the drawbacks.

为什么常卡在 7 级Why people get stuck at 7

  • 三个理由其实是同一个理由换说法。The three reasons are really one reason said three ways.
  • 让步段落说得太多，听起来像改变了立场。The concession goes on too long and sounds like a change of mind.
  • 90 秒没说满，或者最后 20 秒在重复。Does not fill 90 seconds, or repeats for the last 20.

冲 9 级以上的三个动作Three moves to reach 9+

  1. 三个理由从不同角度来：个人、家庭/社会、经济或环境。Take the three reasons from different angles: personal, family or society, money or environment.
  2. 最强的理由放第一或最后，加一个具体例子或数字。Put the strongest reason first or last and give it an example or a number.
  3. 让步只用一句，然后马上用 Even so / On balance 回到立场。Concede in one sentence, then return at once with Even so or On balance.

拿第 1 题对照看Side by side: prompt 1

Do you think grocery stores should charge for plastic bags?

Level 5–65–6 级 · 108 words108 词

Yes, I think grocery stores should charge for plastic bags. I have three reasons.

First, it is good for the environment. Plastic bags are very bad. They stay in the ground for a long time. If people pay, they use fewer bags.

Second, people will bring their own bags. In Vancouver, many stores charge money. Now I have a cloth bag in my car. I always bring it.

Third, it is not expensive. A bag is ten cents. It is not a problem for people.

Some people don't like it. Sometimes I forget my bags too. But I think it is good. So stores should charge for bags.

Level 7–87–8 级 · 161 words161 词

Yes, I think grocery stores should charge for plastic bags, and I have three reasons.

First, it reduces waste. Plastic bags are used for only ten minutes, but they stay in the ground for hundreds of years. If people have to pay, they will use fewer bags.

Second, people will bring their own bags. In Vancouver, many stores already charge, and now I keep a cloth bag in my car and one in my backpack. It became a habit after one week. Now I almost never forget, and I feel good about it every time.

Third, the cost is small. A bag costs about ten cents. That's not a big problem for anyone, and the money can help the store buy paper bags.

Some people say it is annoying, and sometimes I forget my bags too. But I think it's worth it for the environment. That's why I believe stores should charge. It's a small change that makes a big difference.

Level 9+9 级以上 · 189 words189 词

Yes, I firmly believe grocery stores should charge for plastic bags, and I'd like to give three reasons.

First and foremost, it cuts down on waste. A plastic bag is used for maybe ten minutes, yet it can linger in a landfill for centuries. Even a small fee makes people stop and ask themselves whether they really need one, and that alone dramatically reduces how many are handed out.

Secondly, it changes habits remarkably quickly. Here in Vancouver, many stores already charge, and within a week of the rule coming in, I'd trained myself to keep a cloth bag in the car and another in my backpack. These days I hardly ever forget, and it's become second nature.

Thirdly, the cost to shoppers is trivial. At around ten cents a bag, it's hardly a burden on anyone's budget, and stores can put that revenue towards greener alternatives like paper bags.

Admittedly, it can be a nuisance when you forget your bags, and it's happened to me too. But that minor inconvenience is a small price to pay for a cleaner environment. That's why I'm in favour of the charge.

Level 5–6 → 7–8从 5–6 级到 7–8 级

  1. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 Plastic bags are very bad. They stay in the ground for a long time. → Plastic bags are used for only ten minutes, but they stay in the ground for hundreds of years.
    「用十分钟、留几百年」的对比，比 very bad 有力得多。The contrast (ten minutes of use, hundreds of years in the ground) is far stronger than "very bad".
  2. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 Now I have a cloth bag in my car. I always bring it. → It became a habit after one week.
    加上「一周就成习惯」，个人例子直接支持「人们会改变」的观点。"It became a habit after one week" makes the personal example support the point that people change.

Level 7–8 → 9+从 7–8 级到 9 级以上

  1. Vocabulary词汇 First, it reduces waste. → First and foremost, it cuts down on waste.
    first and foremost 标出最重要的理由，cut down on 是地道搭配。"First and foremost" flags the strongest reason, and "cut down on" is a natural collocation.
  2. Content / Coherence内容与连贯 If people have to pay, they will use fewer bags. → Even a small fee makes people stop and ask themselves whether they really need one
    解释收费「为什么」有效（让人停下来想一想），理由有深度。Explaining why a fee works (people stop and think) gives the reason depth.
  3. Listenability好懂程度 Some people say it is annoying, and sometimes I forget my bags too. But I think it's worth it for the environment. → But that minor inconvenience is a small price to pay for a cleaner environment.
    a small price to pay for 是让步后收回观点的经典句型。"A small price to pay for" is the classic way to concede and return to your view.
