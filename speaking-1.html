        官方怎么讲这个任务What the official packs say about this task

        帮对方做一个决定，或为某件事做好准备。准备 30 秒，说 90 秒。Help someone decide something or get ready for something. 30 s to prepare, 90 s to speak.
        • 直接对这个人说话（称呼名字，用 you）。Talk to the person directly: use their name and “you”.
        • 至少给三条建议。Give at least three suggestions.
        • 每条建议都配一个实际的理由或例子。Back each suggestion with a practical reason or example.
        • 每换一条建议，用一个过渡短语提醒听的人（学习包举了三个例子，本页短语库里都有）。Flag each new suggestion with a transition phrase (the pack gives three examples; all of them are in our phrase bank).
        • 语气热心、体贴。Sound helpful and thoughtful.

        Official level 5官方评分 5 级 Speaking Pro Target 5 study pack, p. 9官方 Target 5 学习包第 9 页
        朋友考上了第一志愿大学，但担心付不起学费，给他出主意。A friend got into his first-choice university but is worried about paying the tuition; advise him.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 提出了一个可行的主意（课余打工），还举了几个打工地点。Offers one workable idea (a part-time job) with a few places to work.
        • 用 and、but、so 以及稍长的过渡语串起内容。Links ideas with and, but, so and a couple of longer transitions.
        • 语气友好、支持，说满了时间。Friendly, supportive tone; uses the full time.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 真正展开的只有一条建议，离「至少三条」差得远。Only one suggestion is really developed, well short of three.
        • 有几个说法很别扭，听的人要猜。Several awkward phrases leave the listener guessing.
        • 嗯啊和半句重来打断了流畅，部分发音听起来吃力。Fillers and restarts break the flow; some pronunciation is hard to follow.
        Official level 8官方评分 8 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 9官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 9 页
        朋友想和一位女同事熟起来，但不好意思开口，教他怎么聊、聊什么。A friend wants to get to know a co-worker but is too shy to start; advise him how and what to talk about.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 建议具体：先借共同的项目开口，再约咖啡、午饭。Concrete steps: open with the shared project, then coffee, then lunch.
        • 节奏稳、语调好；语气友好，有收尾。Steady rhythm, good intonation; friendly, with a closing.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 有的想法没展开，前后重复；只用基础连接词。Some ideas are not developed and get repeated; only basic linking words.
        • 几处用法不对，口头禅和自我纠正时不时打断。A few wrong word uses; fillers and self-corrections interrupt at times.
        Official level 9官方评分 9 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 10官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 10 页
        和上面 5 级样本同一道题：学费。Same question as the Level 5 sample above: the tuition.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 细节多而具体：校内住宿、留学生打工的规定、愿意帮他转简历、介绍熟人。Many specific details: campus housing, the work rules for students, offering to pass on his résumé and introduce contacts.
        • 用了不少精准短语和从句；没有长停顿；直接对朋友说，说满时间。Precise phrases and subordinate clauses; no long pauses; speaks straight to the friend for the full time.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 有一处没说清（他到底是不是读过同一所学校）。One point is unclear (did he attend the same school?).
        • 有些词太笼统（「很好」「挺贵」「很有帮助」）。Some words are too general (“really good”, “pretty expensive”, “great help”).

        高分样本多做了什么What the higher-level samples did differently

        • 同一道学费题：5 级只说了「去打工」这一个泛泛的主意；9 级把同一个主意拆成几个能马上去做的具体步骤，还主动提出帮忙。差别不在难词，在「具体」和「完整」。Same tuition question: Level 5 offers one general idea, “get a part-time job”; Level 9 turns the same idea into several concrete steps the friend can act on, and offers help. The gap is not hard words; it is being specific and complete.
        • 连 9 级样本也因为「很好」「挺贵」这类笼统词被点名：能换成数字或名字的地方，就换。Even the Level 9 sample is marked down for vague words like “really good” and “pretty expensive”: wherever a number or a name fits, use it.

        这个任务要你做什么What this task wants

        帮朋友、同事或亲戚做决定，或为某件事做准备。对着对方直接说话。Help a friend, coworker or relative decide something or get ready for something.
        • 直接称呼对方（Hi Sam…），语气友好、愿意帮忙。Speak directly to the person in a helpful tone.
        • 至少给三条建议，每条配一个理由或小例子。At least three suggestions, each with a reason or example.
        • 用 First / Another thing / Finally 把三条分开，听的人才跟得上。Signal each new idea with a transition.

        官方时间：准备 30 秒，说话 90 秒。准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳转并发出提示音，录音自动开始。Official timing: 30 s prep and 90 s speaking. When prep ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear a prompt, and recording starts automatically.

        • 建议要「做得到」：别说「你要努力」，说「每天晚饭后散步二十分钟」。Advice must be "doable": don't say "you must try hard", say "walk for twenty minutes after dinner every day".
        • 三条够了，别讲五条讲不完。时间还剩，就把最后一条的例子讲细。Three tips are enough. Five won't fit. If you have time left, tell the last example in more detail.
        • 不要念稿式的长句；短句 + 停顿比一口气的复杂句得分更稳。Don't use long, memorized-sounding sentences. Short sentences with pauses score more steadily than one long complex sentence.
