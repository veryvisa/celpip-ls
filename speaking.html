评分看什么：四个维度How you are scored: four dimensions

What the rater asks官方问的问题
Content / Coherence内容与连贯想法是否有条理、展开了吗？（想法的数量与质量、顺序、例子和细节）Were my ideas organized and developed? (number and quality of ideas, order, examples and details)
Vocabulary词汇用词是否合适、自然？（选词、范围、准确度）Did I use suitable, natural words and phrases? (word choice, range, accuracy)
Listenability好懂程度别人听起来轻松吗？（节奏、发音、停顿和嗯啊、语法、句式变化）Was it easy to listen to? (rhythm, pronunciation, pauses and 'um's, grammar, sentence variety)
Task Fulfillment完成任务有没有按要求做？（切题、完整、语气得当、把时间用上）Did I follow the instructions? (relevant, complete, right tone, filled the time)
每个任务分别按这四项打分，你的口语级别由 8 个任务综合得出。想拿 5–6 级：先保证「别人听得懂、话说得连贯」，再追求词汇和复杂句。Each task is rated on all four, and your Speaking level combines all eight tasks. For level 5–6, first be easy to understand and organized; then add vocabulary and longer sentences.

官方建议的练法（学习包）How the official study pack suggests practising

  • 每道题录音，然后对照四个维度看哪里好、哪里要改。Record every response, then check it against the four dimensions.
  • 把录音写成文字，花 20–30 分钟改得更强（语法、衔接词、更具体的细节）。考试时不能改，但这样练能长本事。Transcribe a recording and spend 20–30 minutes improving it. You cannot revise on test day, but it builds skill.
  • 有选择的任务（5、6、7）：两个选项都录一遍。For tasks with a choice (5, 6, 7), record both options.
  • 先给自己比 30–60 秒更长的准备时间，再逐渐缩到真实时间。Start with more than 30–60 s of prep time, then shorten it to the real time.
