        看一幅图，讲图里正在发生什么（听的人看不到图）。准备 30 秒，说 60 秒。Look at a picture and say what is happening (the listener cannot see it). 30 s to prepare, 60 s to speak.
        • 先用一句话讲整体。Start with one general sentence about the whole scene.
        • 再挑一些细节讲，不必面面俱到。Then pick some details; you do not need to cover everything.
        • 一点点把画面在对方脑子里搭起来。Build the picture up for the listener piece by piece.
        • 多用描述性的词和短语。Use descriptive words and phrases.
        • 讲人的样子、动作和情绪。Describe people’s appearance, actions and feelings.

        Official level 5官方评分 5 级 Speaking Pro Target 5 study pack, p. 13官方 Target 5 学习包第 13 页
        一家超市里，几家人在买东西、排队结账。A supermarket where several families are shopping and paying.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 先给了整体描述，再讲人和东西；超市相关的词用得准（过道、购物车等）。Gives an overall description first, then people and objects; shop words are accurate (aisle, cart and so on).
        • 发音清楚，节奏还算稳；说满了时间。Clear pronunciation, fairly steady rhythm; uses the full time.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 细节只是列出来，没有展开；有几个说法不准或别扭。Details are listed, not developed; some imprecise or awkward phrases.
        • 简单句掌握得好，但有些搭配不自然。Good control of simple sentences, with some odd constructions.
        Official level 8官方评分 8 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 16官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 16 页
        电影院的售票大厅。A cinema lobby where people buy tickets.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 说出了地点和一些人、物；发音清楚；用了一个较复杂的句子。Names the place and some people and objects; clear pronunciation; tries one longer sentence.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 讲到人物动作时卡住，停顿和重来打乱节奏；想法没展开。Hesitates when describing actions; pauses and restarts break the rhythm; ideas are not developed.
        • 语速慢，时间到了还没讲出足够内容。Slow pace, so not enough gets said before time is up.
        Official level 9官方评分 9 级 Speaking Pro Target 9+ study pack, p. 17官方 Target 9+ 学习包第 17 页
        和上面 5 级样本同一幅超市图。The same supermarket picture as the Level 5 sample.

        What worked做得好的地方

        • 开场就交代规模：多大、忙不忙、大约几个人；再讲精确的小细节（只开了一个收银台、什么水果在打折）。Opens with the size of the scene: how big, how busy, roughly how many people; then precise small details (only one till open, which fruit is on sale).
        • 用从句把细节挂到具体的人身上；组织得好。Uses clauses to attach details to particular people; well organised.

        What held it back拖分的地方

        • 要更高，需要更多更精准的词；最后一句被截断。More precise and varied words would push it higher; the last idea is cut off.

        高分样本多做了什么What the higher-level samples did differently

        • 同一幅超市图：5 级用 and 把一个个动作串起来；9 级先数、先定位（几个人、哪个收银台开着），再用「一个……的男孩」这类从句把细节挂到人身上。听的人因此能在脑子里看到画面。Same supermarket picture: Level 5 strings actions together with and; Level 9 counts and locates first (how many people, which till is open), then hangs details on people with clauses like “a boy who…”. That is what lets the listener see it.
        • 两个高分样本都没有把图讲完。官方本来就说不用讲全，挑三四处讲细更好。Neither high-scoring sample covers the whole picture. The official advice says you do not need to; three or four areas in detail is better.

        这个任务要你做什么What this task wants

        看一幅图，描述画面里发生的事。你需要说「图里有什么」，不必说完所有细节。Look at a picture and say what the people in it are doing.
        • 先说总体（这是一个……的场景），再挑几个细节。Start with a general statement, then pick some details.
        • 按位置说：in the front / on the left / in the background。Use positions to stay organized.
        • 人物的外貌、动作、感受都可以说；不会的词用简单词绕开。Appearance, actions and feelings; use simple words if you lack a word.

        官方时间：准备 30 秒，说话 60 秒。准备时间一到，屏幕自动跳转并发出提示音，录音自动开始。Official timing: 30 s prep and 60 s speaking. When prep ends the screen moves on by itself, you hear a prompt, and recording starts automatically.

        • 不用说完所有东西：挑 4–5 个你能说好的细节，说清楚比说全更重要。You don't need to describe everything: pick 4 to 5 details you can say well. Saying them clearly matters more than saying them all.
        • 按位置走一圈（前景 → 左右 → 背景），不会漏也不会乱。Walk around by position (foreground, left and right, background), so you won't miss anything or get messy.
        • 看不懂的物品用简单词绕：a thing you use to carry food。For objects you don't know, go around with simple words: "a thing you use to carry food".
