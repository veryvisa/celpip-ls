三种题型The three kinds of question

  • 大意：看大局，整段或几个人的想法。例：两位说话人在谈……General meaning: a big-picture question: the whole clip, or ideas from several sentences or speakers. Example: “The two speakers are talking about ___.”
  • 具体信息：某一处说过的一个事实：日期、名字、地点、原因。Specific information: one fact such as a date, name, place, or reason that was said in one spot.
  • 推理：音频没直说，要你推出来，比如感受或接下来会发生什么。probably、most likely 是线索。Inference: a conclusion about something not stated directly, such as a feeling or what will happen next. Words like probably and most likely are clues.
还有一个到处都会遇到的现象：同义替换。正确选项很少和音频用一模一样的词，要练「听到 A，看到 A′ 也能认出来」。One more thing you will meet everywhere: paraphrase. The correct answer rarely uses the same words as the audio. Train yourself to hear A and recognise A′.
