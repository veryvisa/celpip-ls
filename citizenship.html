选一个章节开始。建议先用「EN+中」对照模式把每章过一遍，考前两天切到 EN，只看英文作答，和真考一样。Pick a chapter to start. Go through each chapter in the bilingual mode first; two days before the test switch to EN only, as in the real test.

考试是怎样的How the test works

  • 考试共 20 道选择题和判断题，限时 45 分钟，至少答对 15 题才通过；一般有 3 次机会。The test has 20 multiple-choice and true-or-false questions, takes 45 minutes, and you need at least 15 right to pass. You usually get 3 chances.
  • 考题来自官方学习指南《Discover Canada》，用英文或法文作答。Questions come from the official study guide Discover Canada; you answer in English or French.
  • 签署申请时 18 至 54 岁的人要考；同一年龄段还要交 CLB 4 的听说语言证明，这就是本站另一半「CELPIP 听说」要解决的事。Applicants aged 18 to 54 on the day they sign take the test. The same age group must also prove CLB 4 in listening and speaking, which is what the CELPIP half of this site is for.
