常见陷阱Common question traps

  • 一堆数字Many numbers
    价格、日期、数量。每个数字都和它的标签一起记：「押金 300」。Prices, dates, quantities. Write a number with its label every time: “$300 deposit”.
  • 条件句Conditions
    「周五前签约价格不变」——题目会考条件，不只是事实。“If you sign by Friday, the price stays.” Questions test the condition, not just the fact.
  • 不是 / 除了Not / except
    问「哪项不包括」时，要把每个选项都对照笔记。A question asking what is not included needs every option checked against your notes.
  • 听的人最后选了什么What the listener finally chooses
    听的人会比较几个选项，最后才决定。等最后的决定。The listener compares options and decides at the end. Wait for the final decision.

记笔记方法Note-taking tips

左边写事项，右边写数字和条件；画方框圈出「最后的决定」。Items on the left, numbers and conditions on the right; box the final decision.
  • 用要点和缩写，不写整句；你的笔记没人看，不影响分数。Use short points and abbreviations. Nobody reads your notes and they do not affect your score.
  • 按顺序记：题目的顺序大致和答案在音频里出现的顺序一致。Take notes in order. Questions come in roughly the same order as the answers in the audio.
  • 考前用纸和笔练（敲键盘不是同一种体验），找到你自己的缩写系统。Practise with pen and paper before the test, and build your own abbreviation system.
  • 考场发纸和笔；需要更多纸可以举手要。You get paper and a pen at the test centre; raise your hand for more paper.
