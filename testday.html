考试日：带什么、怎么走流程、卡住怎么办Test day: what to bring, how it runs, what to do when stuck

出门前核对Before you leave home

  1. 带原件证件（和注册时用的是同一个）Original ID (the same one you registered with)
  2. 确认邮件（打印或手机里）Confirmation email, printed or on your phone
  3. 提前 45 分钟到；开考前 15 分钟签到截止Arrive 45 minutes early; check-in closes 15 minutes before the start
  4. 签到前先去洗手间；贵重物品不要带Use the washroom before check-in; leave valuables at home
官方：证件必须是原件、有效、未过期，且必须和注册时用的是同一份（姓名和证件号一致）。复印件和过期证件不能用；不符合会被拒绝入场，不退款、不能转场。Official: your ID must be the original, valid, unexpired, and the same document you registered with (name and number must match). Photocopies and expired documents are not accepted; if your ID does not qualify you will be refused entry with no refund or transfer.

到考场之后会发生什么What happens at the centre

  • 签到：证件会被扫描；你要签名、拍一张照片（脸要完整露出，会印在成绩单上）。Check-in: your ID is scanned, you sign, and you have your picture taken (face fully visible; it is printed on the score report).
  • 除了证件，其他东西都不能带进考场，要放进储物柜或袋子里；手机等电子设备要关机。Only your ID may go into the test room. Everything else goes in a locker or bag, and electronic devices are switched off.
  • 考场会发纸和笔，考完要交回。可能会被要求翻口袋、卷起袖子和裤腿、摘眼镜检查。You get a pen and notepaper, which you return at the end. You may be asked to turn out pockets, roll up sleeves and pantlegs, and remove glasses.
  • 坐下后，不能自己离开座位。需要什么（多一张纸、去洗手间）就举手。Once seated you may not leave your seat unescorted. Raise your hand if you need anything, such as more paper or a washroom break.
  • 考试时钟不会因任何原因停止，包括上洗手间。回来要再做一次安全检查；离开座位超过 20 分钟，考试会被终止。The test clock never stops, even for a washroom break. You will go through another security check on return, and if you are away more than 20 minutes your test is terminated.
  • 你会听到别人说话或打字的声音，官方说这是正常的考场环境，包括别人交答案的声音。You may hear other test takers speaking or typing. The officially described standard condition includes the sound of others submitting responses.
  • 流程的先后顺序在不同考场可能略有不同。The order of procedures may differ between centres.

Official timeHow to use it怎么用
Listening 1–330 s per question听完题就选，别回头纠结上一题。Choose as soon as you hear the question, and don't go back and agonize over the previous one.
Listening 4–6one shared time for all questions先做有把握的；大约每题 30 秒；每题都要答。Do the ones you are sure of first; about 30 seconds per question; answer every question.
Speaking 130 s prep · 90 s准备时写 3 个关键词，不是写句子。During preparation write 3 keywords, not sentences.
Speaking 2, 3, 4, 830 s prep · 60 s开头一句要现成：「A few months ago…」「This picture shows…」。Have your opening sentence ready: "A few months ago..." or "This picture shows...".
Speaking 560 s choose · 60 s prep · 60 s选完立刻想反驳对方方案的一个理由。As soon as you choose, think of one reason to argue against the other person's plan.
Speaking 660 s prep · 60 s先想「问题 → 决定 → 理由」，语气礼貌但坚定。First think "problem, decision, reason", with a polite but firm tone.

耳机和麦克风Headset and microphone

以下是我们的实用建议，不是官方页面写的规定。This is our practical advice, not a rule stated on the official pages.
  • 正式开始前，调好耳机音量；觉得声音太小、太吵或有杂音，马上举手告诉考场人员，不要等到听力开始后。Before the real questions start, set your headset volume. If the sound is too quiet, noisy or broken, raise your hand and tell the staff right away, not after the Listening begins.
  • 口语时用正常音量说话。官方提醒：不要太大声也不要太小声，这会影响「好懂程度」；背景有一点杂音没关系，只要你用正常音量，评分员能听清。Speak at your normal volume. The official study pack warns that too loud or too soft hurts Listenability. Some noise in the room is fine; the raters will hear you clearly at your usual volume.
  • 本站的练习区就是为了让你习惯：自己的声音被录下来，别人也在旁边说话。可以在有一点点背景声的地方练一次。The practice mode on this site helps you get used to hearing yourself recorded. Try it once with a little background noise.
  • 遇到任何设备问题，当场告诉考场人员；考后 24 小时内还可以在 CELPIP 账号里报告考试日问题（官方）。Report any equipment problem to the centre staff on the spot. Officially you can also report a test-day issue in your CELPIP account within 24 hours after the test.

卡住了怎么办What to do when you are stuck

  • 听力：没听懂的题不要停下来。选一个最像的答案，进入下一题；答错不扣分，空着一定吃亏。Listening: never freeze on one question. Pick the most likely answer and move on. Wrong answers are not penalised; a blank always costs you.
  • 听力第 4–6 部分：先做有把握的，再回头做难的；注意看剩余时间。Listening Parts 4–6: do the sure questions first, then the hard ones, and watch the clock.
  • 口语：说错了不要重来，直接说「What I mean is …」接着说。时间很宝贵，流畅比完美更重要。Speaking: if you make a mistake, do not restart. Say “What I mean is …” and continue. Time is short; flow matters more than perfection.
  • 口语：脑子空白时，先说模板的开头一句（本站每个任务页都有），再从最简单的一个例子开始。Speaking: if your mind goes blank, say the opening line of the template (every task page has one) and start with your easiest example.
  • 缺一个词：用简单的话绕过去，例如 “a thing you use for …”。Missing a word: go around it with simple words, for example “a thing you use for …”.
  • 说完了还有时间：加一个例子或一个感受，不要沉默到结束。Finished early: add one more example or a feeling. Do not sit in silence until the timer ends.

考后After the test
